## Second redesign: the board, the pipeline, more chips (9 Sept, night)

The client rejected the dot strip and the dot matrix ("small and hard to navigate") and, after two rounds of image mockups, chose these. **Export Controls, "What the rules did"** (`Geo` in `src/app.js`, `src/modules/controls.css`, `data/control-flows.json`): the page is "the board", nine things that move across the chokepoint (advanced AI chips, US tools, allied controls, HBM, US goods to listed firms, critical minerals, foreign chips inside China, chips to the rest of the world, money and tariffs), each with its state as of a date (open, licence, case by case, shut, policy), the label of the move that set it and its move count. **By subject** (default) opens each row into its own history with a pill beside every state-changing move; a year scrubber sets the as-of date (arrow keys work); **By date** keeps the chronology with the filter chips and Show full summaries, and on desktop a compact board rides sticky at the right and flips as the entry at the reading line changes. States come from `control-flows.json` (`states[]` per flow, each citing an event `id`; every timeline event carries `id` and `flows`), written by an editor from the 43 summaries (`qa/flows-data.md`); a state carries the tightest gate standing, not just the latest rule. ## Worker lessons (for whoever orchestrates next)

- Forbid subagents in worker prompts (the concurrency cap counts them and they stall).
- The WebSearch budget is shared and exhausts early; tell workers to use WebFetch on primary sources and Google News RSS.
- Tell workers to save drafts early; API connection drops kill agents silently. Run a background auto-commit loop while they write.
- Give each worker its own files; when two need the same JSON, split it (as with `landscape-layouts.json`).
- The hidden browser pane paints only the first frame and refuses screenshots when not displayed; verify layout with DOM measurements through `javascript_tool`, and use `?only=<chapter-id>` to load a single chapter.

Concentration along the chain

Twenty segments in chain order. The dark bar is the top country's share, the light bar the second; the number at the right is the HHI. Amber where the HHI passes 5,000.

Computed live from data/chokepoint-matrix.json (shares by country, headquarters basis; 20 rows). HHI sums squared country shares including Other, so it is a floor. With the basis field in place, each bar could carry a small letter (M, D, E, R) for measured, derived, estimate or relayed, and the year. This is the chart the site's text promises and the score never gave: it makes photomasks and metrology comparable at a glance.

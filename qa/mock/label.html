Concentration is computed from the matrix row: HHI over country shares (0 to 10,000) and the top-2 share, with the year and the basis (measured, derived, estimate, relayed). Substitutability is a 4-band rating against four written tests: a qualified second source exists (easy); a second source exists but is not qualified for the leading node (moderate); no second source, replaceable by a well-funded state actor in 5 to 10 years (hard); physics- or IP-limited, 10+ years (very hard). The actor on the clock is always the same: a well-funded state or firm standing up an alternative. Each card shows its scoring line.

# Decision (17 Sept 2026): Ledger, in garnet, with Folio's specimen caption

The author chose **Ledger** (`qa/brand-redesign/ledger/`, its README and `ledger.css`) as the site's design, with these changes:

- **Accent: garnet `#7a1f2b`** (text pairing `#611722`, soft fill `rgba(122,31,43,0.09)`), replacing Ledger's oxide red everywhere the accent lives: the chosen tab and state, the chapter numbers, the ledger column's fill and tick, the Extreme and Very hard ratings, citation numbers, links, the board's presumption-of-denial pills, Critical firms, the Extreme band. Ground stays Ledger's warm white `#f7f6f2` with blue-black ink `#14181f`. Dark theme: a garnet lifted for the dark ground (about `#c9536a`, check contrast) on Ledger's dark tokens.
- **Specimen caption from Folio** (`qa/brand-redesign/folio/folio.css` lines 76 to 85, and the final mock `qa/brand-redesign/final/site/index.html` style block `#final-specimen`): the canvas first, then a short centered 28 px rule, then "SPECIMEN 04 · LITHOGRAPHY" in tracked capitals (Jost 400, 10.5 px, 0.28 em), then the caption centered in Jost 12.5 px at a 40ch measure. On phones the base bottom strip stays.
- **Specimens are true to color, not garnet.** The author: "I don't want this garnet to be across all specimens, which should ideally be true to color. If some specimens should be white, yellow, blue, green, or whatever, to be authentic to the actual machinery, keep them those colors." So the accent points inside each 3D specimen take the color the real object has, chosen per specimen and declared in its file; the site's garnet is for the interface only. The rendered reference is `qa/brand-redesign/final/` (Ledger carmine); swap carmine for garnet in your head.

Everything else in Ledger's README holds: Fraunces display (weight 300 at the large optical size for numerals, 400 for titles), Source Serif 4 body at 18.5 px, Instrument Sans for labels and data, measure 34 em, hairlines, square corners, no shadows, no grain, the ruled ledger column on the left (home: chapter numbers and a hairline that fills as you read; chapters: the number and the fifteen-stage index turned vertical), the chokepoint card as a ruled statement. American spelling, no em dashes, body text at 4.5:1 or better, the accent at 4.5:1 for small text.

# Brand redesign brief (17 Sept 2026)

The author's words: the site "is a very easy giveaway for an AI-generated website". They want a visually stunning, clean, minimalist site, and they will judge four candidate directions. They pointed at https://www.themozi.org/chapters/ch01.html as an example of restraint and craft (captured at `qa/brand-redesign/ref-mozi.png`), and then added: "do not be too biased and anchored on the reference website." So: match its level of care, not its look. Do not reuse its palette (paper, cinnabar, bronze), its faces (Cormorant, Spectral) or its centered-title layout as a starting point.

## What the site is
Chip Supply Chain (chipsupplychain.org): seventeen chapters on how AI chips are made, with a 3D point-cloud specimen beside the text on every chapter, a chokepoint card per stage, data pages (35 chips compared, 220 suppliers on a world map, an export-controls board and timeline), and a glossary. Readers: policy staff, researchers, students. The current look (`qa/brand-redesign/current-home.png`, tokens at the top of `src/styles.css`): wafer-grey ground `#edeff0`, graphite ink, amber accent `#c98a12`, IBM Plex Sans Condensed display, Newsreader body, IBM Plex Mono labels. Everything is built from CSS custom properties in `src/styles.css` `:root` (colors, three font stacks, `--measure`, `--col` the text/stage split) plus per-module CSS in `src/modules/*.css`. The specimen renderer reads `--stage-ink` and `--stage-accent` (RGB triplets) for its point colors, and `--c1` to `--c8` are the eight country colors on the Suppliers page; severity uses `--accent` (amber) for Extreme and Critical.

## What each designer delivers
Work only in your own folder `qa/brand-redesign/<key>/`. Do not edit any site source file. Copy `dist/` to `qa/brand-redesign/<key>/site/` (it is one HTML file plus `data/`, `cards/`, share pages and images), serve it on your own port (`python3 -m http.server <port> --directory qa/brand-redesign/<key>/site`, ports are assigned per designer), and restyle by editing the copy's HTML: swap the Google Fonts link, and append a `<style>` block at the end of `<head>` that overrides the `:root` tokens and whatever selectors the direction needs (masthead, wordmark, chapter head, plate, walk head, buttons, hairlines, the home overview box). Real fonts only, from Google Fonts, with fallback stacks. Do not touch the point-cloud code; retheme it through `--stage-ink` and `--stage-accent`.

Captures with `python3 build/shoot.py <url> <out.png> --width W --height H --wait 6 [--full] [--eval "..."]` (force light or your own theme with `--eval "document.documentElement.setAttribute('data-theme','light')"`; the default is whatever the copy sets): (1) home top at 1440 by 900 (`#/`), (2) the Lithography chapter head with its chokepoint card at 1440 by 1100 (`#lithography`, scroll to it with `--eval "document.getElementById('lithography').scrollIntoView()"`), (3) the Suppliers page top at 1440 by 900 (`#/suppliers`), (4) home at 390 by 844 (phone), (5) one dark-theme capture of the home top if your direction has a dark theme. Also a one-page `README.md`: the direction in one paragraph (what world it draws on, why it suits a reference on chipmaking), the token sheet (6 to 8 named hex values, the three font stacks, the measure), what changed beyond tokens, and an honest note on what would need real work to ship (any layout the override could not reach).

## Rules
No em dashes anywhere; American spelling. Accessibility holds: body text at 4.5:1 or better on its ground, the accent usable for small text at 4.5:1 or paired with a darker ink variant, country colors still distinguishable. Keep every piece of content and every control; this is a reskin, not a redesign of the information. Avoid the looks that read as generated: warm cream with terracotta and a serif display; near-black with one acid-green or vermilion pop; a purple-to-blue gradient hero; Inter or Space Grotesk as the safe face; emoji; everything centered; rounded cards with a colored rail on every block. Make one deliberate choice a reader would remember, and keep everything around it quiet.

Stage 05 of 15

Lithography

The most concentrated stage in the chain. One firm in the Netherlands builds every EUV scanner on Earth, and it shipped 48 of them in 2025.

1,343 words / 6 minSpecimen: EUV scanner

In short

One firm, in one Dutch town, using mirrors from one German optics house, gates the resolution of every leading-edge AI chip. The tool count is small enough to track by hand: 48 EUV systems in 20251 and ten High-NA systems in the field worldwide9. Export controls have cut China’s share of ASML system sales from 41% in 2024 toward a guided 20% in 202623, and China’s answer is low-volume immersion DUV plus an EUV prototype that has not yet made a chip. Nothing on the horizon changes the shape of this bottleneck before 2030.

Why substitution is slow
ASML built its first EUV prototype in 2001 and needed nearly two decades and billions of euros before commercial chips shipped in 2019, and it still depends on Zeiss optics that no one else can make.
China

No EUV has ever been sold to a customer in China. A Shenzhen prototype built by former ASML engineers generated EUV light in early 2025 but has not made a working chip. A Shanghai state firm, Aishengna, began building domestic immersion DUV in 2026 at single-digit annual volumes.

United States

No domestic scanner maker. Washington controls access through Dutch licensing, US-origin subsystems and entity-list rules.

In plain terms

Lithography is photographic printing, shrunk. A stencil of one layer of the circuit is held up to a lamp, and the machine projects that image onto a silicon wafer coated in a light-sensitive film, four times smaller than the stencil. Finer circuits need shorter-wavelength light, and the shortest light now in use is absorbed by air and by glass, so the whole machine runs in a vacuum and every lens has to be a mirror. Only one company has ever built such a machine, which is why a single Dutch firm is a chokepoint.

Every transistor in an AI accelerator gets its shape from a machine built by one company. ASML sold 327 lithography systems in 2025, 48 of them extreme ultraviolet, and nobody else sells EUV at all1. No other stage in this chain has a single point of failure this sharp.

How it works

Resolution follows one relation: the smallest printable half-pitch equals a process factor times the wavelength, divided by the numerical aperture of the optics. Shorter wavelength and larger aperture both buy resolution. Aperture depends on the refractive index above the wafer, so flooding that gap with ultrapure water lifts ASML’s deep ultraviolet systems to 1.354.

# Corrections from the compute governance read

eCFR at 1 September 2026.

**1. Rest-of-the-world flow.** All four states moved from `open` to `case`, on 742.6(a)(6)(iii)(A): "A license is required to export, reexport, or transfer (in-country) items specified in ECCNs ... 3A090.a ... 4A090.a ... to or within any destination worldwide." AIA covers the nineteen destinations in supplement no. 5 (740.27(a)); LPP covers "up to 26,900,000 cumulative total processing performance ... to a single ultimate consignee" elsewhere (740.29(a)); review is approval-presumed for the nineteen (A)(2), capped at 790,000,000 TPP per country to 2027 (B)(1). `what`, `route` and cites follow. The May 2025 labels say the text survived, on 91 FR 43034: "BIS is only enforcing this license requirement to destinations in Country Groups D:1, D:4, or D:5 ... and to entities headquartered in ... D:5 or Macau." `gist` and `summary` rewritten on the four matching timeline events.

**2. Glossary.** LPP: "never bound anyone" gone; 740.27 to 740.29 and supplement no. 5 stand, source notes ending 90 FR 5309. HBM: the 3.3 figure is now scoped, on the CCL entry "RS applies to 3A090.c ... To or within Macau or a destination specified in Country Group D:5". Allied shipments need no licence at any density; 740.25(c) governs co-packaging inside China. ACA now names the 3A090.a datacentre limit; NAC needed none. New entry "compute threshold": EO 14110 s.4.2(b)(i), "greater than 10^26 integer or floating-point operations"; ECCN 4E091, "'Parameters' for an artificial intelligence model trained utilizing 10^26 or more 'operations'"; AI Act Article 51(2), "greater than 10^25".

**3. HBM flow and chapter 10.** 742.6(b)(10)(ii): "There is a presumption of approval ... for entities neither headquartered in nor whose ultimate parent company is headquartered in ... Macau or a destination specified in Country Group D:5. There is a presumption of denial policy for all other license applications." Status stays `denial`; label, `what` and the chapter sentence carry the split, with the eCFR as second cite.

**4. Chapter 17.** Photoresist is Japan 78.4 percent, 2023, and wafers 53 percent, 2023, credited to METI's December 2025 deck. BIS announced the H200 policy on 13 January 2026, and 91 FR 1685 reads "The effective date of this rule is January 15, 2026"; both now appear.

**5. Additions.** Cloud and remote access, on 734.13, on the January 2026 certification "necessary to prevent unauthorized remote access from end users described in paragraph (dd)(1)(iv)", and on the diffusion rule's red flag 28, 4E091 and the VEU clause that API and inference access "are not prohibited".

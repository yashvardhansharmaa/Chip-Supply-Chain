## The pause control (2.2.2)
Stopping the turn after one revolution does not satisfy 2.2.2. Moving content needs a control unless it stops within 5 seconds, and one revolution at 0.11 rad/s takes about 57 seconds. A 5-second turn would drop the idle motion the design is built on. So the fix is a button, the smallest control that keeps the motion.
- **Laptop.** A 24 px hairline square sits at the end of the plate tag ("SPECIMEN 04 · LITHOGRAPHY ❚❚"), in the tag's ink-2. It sits under the stage, beside the caption.
- **Phone.** A 28 px square sits over the thumbnail's lower left corner. When the strip is expanded, it moves to the canvas's top left.
- **Behavior.** The label switches between "Pause the turn" and "Resume the turn". The choice is kept in `localStorage` (`cp-still`), and every storage call is guarded. Reduced motion never turns the specimen, so those readers get no button.
- **Code.** `Stage.loop` reads `Stage.still` (one condition on the speed builder's line).

## Not done, and why
- **Ghost buttons and the board's year cells** keep their hairline edges. Their text names them, and their states show by fill or a garnet edge.
- **The Glossary search** keeps `:focus { outline: none; border-color: var(--ink) }`. That is the readers builder's rule, and the ink edge on focus differs from the new resting edge by 4.4:1.
- **Not in this brief:** #10 tooltips, #12 table focus, and the other #5 counts (all readers). #13 target size: axe's remaining `target-size` counts are the chapter rail. #14 focus after a tab change, #15 to #24, and a skip link for `summary/`.
- **Two partly covered stops at the bottom edge on desktop** (a term on the last line) are still in view. 2.4.11 at AA only forbids a stop that is fully hidden.
- **Atlas stops on phones** are 9 px dots with no number, so the phone contrast reading (1.5 to 1.7) measures hidden text. The `opacity: 0.5` there is left as it was.
- **For the coordinator: AI Chips' own rule.** The chips.css rule `html:has(section[data-view="landscape"]:not([hidden]))` sets `scroll-padding-top` at all times and outranks `html.kb`. Its `.cx-h`, `.cx-note` and `.cx-tablewrap` also carry `scroll-margin-top` with the masthead in it. A native `scrollIntoView` to those three would land a masthead and bar lower than intended. Focus is never hidden either way.

# Per-chapter URLs and social cards (11 September 2026; cards redesigned as "Plate" on 18 September)

## What was built

The site is one HTML file with hash routes, so a pasted link used to unfurl as the home page whatever it pointed at. Now every chapter and every view has a real URL of its own that unfurls with its own title, text and card on Slack, LinkedIn, X and Substack, and then sends the reader on to the page.

| Piece | Where | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Unfurl pages | `build/modules/social.js` (auto-loaded by `build/build.js`) writes `dist/<slug>/index.html` | One small static page per chapter (slug = chapter id, `lithography/`) and per view (`home/`, `intro/`, `c/chain/`, `c/chips/`, `c/suppliers/`, `c/export-controls/`, `c/index/`): 24 pages. Each carries `<title>`, `<meta name="description">` (the chapter's first plain sentence, or the view's dek, at most 160 characters), `<link rel="canonical">` to the hash URL on the live site, `og:site_name`, `og:type` (article for chapters, website for views), `og:title`, `og:description`, `og:url`, `og:image` (absolute, with width, height and alt), `twitter:card summary_large_image`, `twitter:title`, `twitter:description`, `twitter:image`; then, after the tags, a `<meta http-equiv="refresh" content="0;url=…">` and a `location.replace` to the hash route. The body shows the eyebrow, the title, the plain paragraph and a "Read the chapter" link, so the page is not empty for a crawler. |
| Card images | `build/cards.py` renders `build/assets/cards/<slug>.png`, with `build/cards-art.js` dumping the specimen clouds; `social.js` copies the folder to `dist/cards/` | 1200 x 630 PNG per page in the "Plate" direction chosen from `qa/cards-redesign-2/light/` on 18 September: the page itself at card size. The site's light tokens from `qa/brand-build.md` (warm white `#f7f6f2`, ink `#14181f`, second ink `#3b424c`, muted `#5c636d`, garnet `#7a1f2b` as the only accent), no gradient and no vignette. On the right, in a 600 by 500 box at `right: 36px; top: 30px`, the chapter's own specimen: the point cloud the page shows, drawn in ink with its accent points in the colors the specimen declares for the light ground, at the tilt the site opens it at, over the centered 28 px rule and a tracked plate line in Jost ("SPECIMEN 04 · LITHOGRAPHY", the number in garnet). The left column (500 px from x = 72) is the chapter head reduced to its essentials: the eyebrow "CHIP SUPPLY CHAIN | CHAPTER 04 · STAGE 4 OF 15" in Instrument Sans with a hairline tick (chapters 16 and 17 say "THE ECONOMICS" and "THE CONTEST"); the title in Fraunces at 116 px (opsz 144, SOFT and WONK at 0); the two ratings as garnet-ruled tags in the site's `.ptag` style ("EXTREME CONCENTRATION", "VERY HARD TO SUBSTITUTE", from the manifest's `tags`; chapters 16 and 17 carry none); and the lede in Source Serif 4 at 25 px, at most five lines: the first two sentences of the chapter's `oneLiner`, the second cut to its first clause when it runs past 120 characters. A view swaps the eyebrow for the wordmark in small caps with the italic tagline ("Chip Supply Chain  Every stage, and who controls it"), carries no tags, and shows its dek. The home card is the site's hero: "How AI chips are made" over the GPU die, with the meta description's sentence as its dek. The foot carries the domain. Sizes run 32 to 103 KB, all under the 150 KB limit. |
| Main page head | `src/template.html` lines 8 to 21 | Canonical, `og:site_name`, `og:type website`, `og:title`, `og:description` (the existing meta description), `og:url`, `og:image` (`cards/home.png`), `twitter:card summary_large_image` and the Twitter title, description and image. |
| Page list | `manifest()` exported from `build/modules/social.js` | The single list of slugs, eyebrows, titles, lines, descriptions and routes. It reads `content/*.md` and `data/suppliers.json` itself, so `cards.py` calls it through `node -e` and the card and the page never disagree. |

Design notes. The art is rendered at build time, not copied from the mock folder: `cards.py` runs `build/cards-art.js` once per specimen, which loads `src/kit.js` and `specimens/<name>.js` in Node, builds the cloud with `PC.build` and writes the float32 rows (x, y, z, w; w at or above 2 marks an accent point, above 3 a second accent) with the tilt the specimen declares and its colors resolved for the light ground (`PC.resolveColors(s, false)`, the same colors the page shows: the EUV beam in pale blue-violet, the die's copper strip, the HBM cube's copper base); `render_art()` in `cards.py` then projects the cloud with Pillow the way `qa/cover/render.py` does, far to near, and saves a transparent PNG that the card's HTML places in the art box on the bare ground. Ink points run from alpha 28 to 140 so the cloud reads as the page's gray stipple on the warm white; accent points are 1.4 times as strong. A chapter's specimen is the `specimen` field of its front matter, read from `content/*.md`; a view takes one from `VIEW_SPECIMEN` in `cards.py`: the GPU die for home, the silicon ingot and wafer for the introduction (where the chain starts), the exploded CoWoS package for The Chain (every part that comes together), the wafer-scale package for AI Chips, the globe of chokepoint towns for Suppliers and Export Controls, the wafer for the Glossary. A chapter's plate line names the chapter ("Specimen 04 · Lithography"); a view's names its specimen with the label from `LABEL` and the number of the chapter that owns it ("Specimen 01 · GPU die, one reticle field"), or the label alone for the two specimens no chapter owns (the wafer-scale processor, the 300 mm wafer). `FRAME` holds a zoom per specimen where the default (0.92 of the box) leaves a tall one small or, for the globe, lets its pins run off the top. The fit script (`FIT_JS`) shrinks the title in 2 px steps (down to 56 px) while it runs past two lines or past the column, then while the whole text column overflows (the lede is `flex: none`, so a squeezed column shows as overflow instead of a silently clipped lede), then shrinks the lede in 1 px steps (down to 20 px) until five lines hold it. So "Chemicals, Gases and Photoresist" sets at 78 px, "The Economics of AI Chips" at 86 px, "Lithography" at 110 px and the one-word view titles at the full 116 px; no lede needed shrinking. It also asks `document.fonts` to load each face the template uses before measuring, and a card whose faces did not arrive is loaded again before it is flagged. Every PNG is quantized to 256 colors without dithering; the size comes from the dots, not their number, so the cloud is drawn without supersampling (crisp 1 to 2 px dots, as on the page), and the script thins the cloud and draws the card again if one is still over 150 KB (none is at the default density of 0.8; the largest is Memory and HBM at 103 KB). The manifest carries `lede` (the chapter's `oneLiner`) and `tags` for the cards beside `line`, which the unfurl page still uses in `og:image:alt`. `og:url` is the static page's own address, not the hash URL: Facebook's and LinkedIn's crawlers re-fetch `og:url` as the canonical, and pointing it at the main page would replace the chapter's tags with the home page's. The `<link rel="canonical">` still points at the hash URL, as asked, for search engines. One lesson from the port: the template is a Python `%` format over one dict, so a text placeholder must not share a name with a CSS token (`line` once did, and a lede with an apostrophe then broke the stylesheet and set the title in the fallback sans); the rule colors are `rule_line` and `rule_line2` for that reason.

Descriptions. Whole sentences are packed while they fit in 160 characters. Two chapters (Lithography, Deposition and Etch) open with a sentence longer than that; the description keeps the clause before the colon and closes it with a full stop ("Lithography prints the pattern of a circuit onto the wafer.").

## How to regenerate the cards

Cards are not rendered on every build (the build only copies them, and warns if one is missing). After a chapter title, band, plain paragraph, view dek or specimen changes:

```
python3 build/cards.py            # all 24 cards, about a minute
python3 build/cards.py --only lithography,home   # a subset
python3 build/cards.py --keep     # keeps the temporary HTML, clouds and art PNGs for inspection, prints the folder
node build/build.js               # copies build/assets/cards/ to dist/cards/ and rewrites dist/
```

`cards.py` needs Chrome at `/Applications/Google Chrome.app`, node (for the manifest and for `build/cards-art.js`), the `websocket-client` package (already used by `build/shoot.py`), numpy and Pillow for the art and the quantize step, and a network connection for Google Fonts (Fraunces, Source Serif 4, Instrument Sans, Jost). It prints one line per card with the size, the specimen, the final title size and line count, the final lede size and the number of points drawn, and flags OVERFLOW, a size over the limit, or FONTS with the faces that did not load. If a font does not load it prints a warning and the cards fall back to system fonts; re-run with the network up. `CARDS_DEBUG=1` prints each card's font-face statuses. To change a view's specimen edit `VIEW_SPECIMEN`; to add a specimen add its plate-line label to `LABEL` and, if it needs one, a zoom to `FRAME`. The title, tags, lede and eyebrow all come from `manifest()` in `build/modules/social.js`, so a copy change there (or in a chapter's front matter) reaches the card and the unfurl page together. The DevTools port is 9334; the mock generators under `qa/cards-redesign-2/` use their own ports.

for s in lithography chemicals-gases-and-photoresist export-controls; do
  curl -s "http://localhost:8841/$s/index.html" | grep -E "<title>|canonical|og:|twitter:|refresh"

curl -sI http://localhost:8841/cards/lithography.png | head -1

## Live checks after deploy

Once `dist/` is pushed, paste `https://yashvardhansharmaa.github.io/chokepoints/lithography/` into a Slack message, LinkedIn's Post Inspector (linkedin.com/post-inspector), X's card validator or a Substack draft. Each should show the Lithography card and the first plain sentence. LinkedIn and Facebook cache a URL for about a week; re-scrape from the inspector after changing a card. A reader opening the URL lands on `#lithography` inside The Chain.

## Open

- A `sitemap.xml` listing the 24 URLs, and structured data (`Article` per chapter), are the natural next step for search engines; not built here.
- The description for a chapter whose plain paragraph opens with a very short sentence (chapter 3: "Photoresist is photographic film for chips.") is that sentence alone when the next one does not fit in 160 characters. Acceptable, but an editor may prefer a hand-written description field in the front matter.
- Cards are rendered at 1x (1200 x 630). A 2x render would be crisper on retina previews but four times the size; not needed for unfurls.

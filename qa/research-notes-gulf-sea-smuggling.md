# Research notes: where the chips go (Gulf, Southeast Asia, smuggling, chip-tracking law)

Verified facts gathered September 2026 for the Geopolitics essay. **[F]** = page fetched and read; **[H]** = headline and date confirmed via a news index only.

## UAE
- 5 GW UAE–US AI Campus led by G42, announced 15 May 2025; Stargate UAE is a 1 GW cluster inside it. [F] https://www.g42.ai/resources/news/g42-lead-consortium-us-partners-build-5gw-uae-us-ai-campus
- The "500,000 chips per year" figure has no fetchable primary source; treat as unverified. G42 had sought 100,000 per year; the November 2025 authorization was 35,000 GB300-class chips, enough for roughly 100 MW. [F] https://www.justsecurity.org/150631/the-trump-administration-throws-gulf-ai-a-lifeline/
- 19–20 Nov 2025: BIS authorized G42 and HUMAIN to buy up to 35,000 GB300 chips each under security and reporting conditions. [F] https://www.middleeastainews.com/p/us-approves-up-to-70000-advanced ; G42's "Regulated Technology Environment" framework: [F] https://www.g42.ai/resources/news/g42-receives-us-approval-advanced-ai-chip-exports-enabling-full-scale-deployment-trusted-ai-infrastructure
- 8 May 2026: UAE ambassador says the first batch of advanced chips has been delivered; first 200 MW to come online soon; $1 trillion invested, $1.4 trillion committed over the next decade. No chip count given. [F] https://www.uae-embassy.org/news/ambassador-al-otaiba-delivers-remarks-scsp-ai-expo-uae-us-ai-partnership
- 14 July 2026 (effective 10 July): BIS final rule moving the UAE from Country Groups D:3/D:4 to A:5, opening License Exception STA for the UAE government and listed entities (G42 and Core42 until 6 April 2027, plus Amazon, Apple, Google, Meta, Microsoft, OpenAI, Oracle, xAI) for ECCNs 3A090 and 4A090. No quantity caps in the rule. Saudi Arabia not mentioned. [F] https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/07/14/2026-14132/enhanced-favorable-treatment-for-the-united-arab-emirates-under-the-export-administration
- Congressional pushback on the UAE decision, Aug 2026 (Meeks and Kamlager-Dove letter to Lutnick, 20 Aug; Warren, 5 Aug). [H]

## Saudi Arabia / HUMAIN
- Announced May 2025: 18,000 GB300s, "several hundred thousand" over five years, up to 500 MW. [F] https://vision2030.ai/analysis/nvidia-gpus-saudi-arabia-ai-chips-export-controls/
- 35,000 chips authorized 19 Nov 2025; first Nvidia GPU shipment arrived 30–31 Dec 2025, quantity undisclosed. [F] https://www.agbi.com/ai/2025/12/humain-receives-first-shipment-of-fast-nvidia-ai-chips/
- AMD–Cisco–HUMAIN JV: 100 MW phase one in 2026, up to 1 GW by 2030; MI355X live in production by 31 Aug 2026, next phase up to 250 MW from 2027. [F] https://newsroom.cisco.com/c/r/newsroom/en/us/a/y2026/m08/amd-cisco-and-humain-expand-saudi-arabia-ai-infrastructure.html
- Reality check: 467 MW of energized data-centre capacity in Saudi Arabia as of Q1 2026 against a 1.5 GW national target for 2030; HUMAIN's 6.6 GW is a corporate pipeline. [F] https://vision2030.ai/analysis/saudi-data-centre-capacity-megawatts/
- As of 31 July 2026: "The UAE has a rule. Saudi Arabia has a licence." [F] https://vision2030.ai/geopolitics/us-chip-export-controls-saudi-uae-divergence/

## The AI Diffusion Rule and its replacement
- Published 15 Jan 2025 (doc. 2025-00636). [F] https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2025/01/15/2025-00636/framework-for-artificial-intelligence-diffusion
- Rescission announced 13 May 2025 with three guidance items; BIS promised a replacement rule with no timeline. [F] https://www.bis.gov/press-release/department-commerce-announces-rescission-biden-era-artificial-intelligence-diffusion-rule-strengthens
- A Federal Register full-text search finds no formal rescission and no tiered replacement rule through 9 Sept 2026. What replaced it: the 15 Jan 2026 rule moving H200/MI325X-class chips for China to case-by-case review with a 50% TPP certification and third-party testing. [F] https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/01/15/2026-00789/revision-to-license-review-policy-for-advanced-computing-commodities ; explainer [F] https://www.mayerbrown.com/en/insights/publications/2026/01/administration-policies-on-advanced-ai-chips-codified
- The 25% revenue share on approved China H200 sales rests on secondary reporting only.
- Affiliates ("50%") rule published 30 Sept 2025, suspended for a year on 10 Nov 2025. [F] https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2025/11/12/2025-19846/one-year-suspension-of-expansion-of-end-user-controls-for-affiliates-of-certain-listed-entities

## Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand
- Malaysia ~13% of global assembly, test and packaging (Z2Data, Dec 2025). [F] https://www.z2data.com/insights/which-countries-does-the-world-rely-on-for-chip-assembly-packaging
- 14 July 2025: MITI permit directive under Section 12 of the Strategic Trade Act 2010 for US-origin AI chips, with 30-day advance notification. [F] https://sanctionsnews.bakermckenzie.com/malaysia-introduces-new-export-control-directive-for-advanced-ai-chips/
- 5 June 2026 (announced 26 June): Malaysian Customs seized 72 servers with AI chips worth RM52.92 million at KLIA (Reuters reports $13 million). [F] https://www.thestar.com.my/news/nation/2026/06/26/customs-dept-seizes-72-servers-with-ai-chips-worth-rm53mil
- Nvidia FY2025 10-K: Singapore billing location $23.68bn of $130.50bn revenue (18.2%); FY2026 10-K no longer breaks Singapore out. [F] https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581025000023/R85.htm ; https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000021/R84.htm
- Singapore fraud case: $390 million of transactions (March 2025); fourth person charged April 2026; four firms charged and S$56 million of assets seized 1 July 2026. [H]
- Megaspeed probe (Oct–Dec 2025). [H]

- 20 Nov 2025: Florida case, 400 A100s exported, H100/H200 shipments disrupted, $3.89 million in PRC wires. [F] https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/us-citizens-and-chinese-nationals-arrested-exporting-artificial-intelligence-technology
- 25 March 2026: Thailand front-company case, 750 servers (~$170 million). [F] https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/chinese-national-and-two-us-citizens-charged-conspiring-smuggle-artificial-intelligence
- Applied Materials $252 million penalty (Feb 2026); Cadence $95 million (July 2025); BIS ~$420 million in penalties over 12 months; Supermicro co-founder arrested over an alleged $2.5 billion scheme. [F] https://fortune.com/2026/05/13/nvidia-chip-smuggling-china-russia-iran-export-controls-supermicro/
- House Select Committee: a median 140,000 chips smuggled to PRC entities in 2024. [F] https://chinaselectcommittee.house.gov/media/press-releases/protecting-us-tech-china-committee-and-bipartisan-bicameral-leaders-unite-to-stop-ccp-ai-chip-smuggling

## Chip-tracking legislation
- Chip Security Act S.1705 (Cotton, 8 May 2025): location verification within 180 days of enactment for 3A090/4A090-class chips. [F] https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/BILLS-119s1705is/xhtml/BILLS-119s1705is.html
- GAIN AI Act dropped from the FY2026 NDAA (Dec 2025). [H]
- House Foreign Affairs passed the Chip Security Act 42–0 on 26 March 2026. [F] https://huizenga.house.gov/news/documentsingle.aspx?DocumentID=404259
- Senate FY2027 NDAA carries Chip Security, AI OVERWATCH and MATCH Acts (14 July 2026); not enacted as of Sept 2026. [F] https://ari.us/senate-ndaa-takes-major-step-to-strengthen-ai-chip-export-controls/
- Nvidia: "No backdoors, no kill switches" (5 Aug 2025) [F] https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/no-backdoors-no-kill-switches-no-spyware/ ; then built location-verification software (Reuters, 10 Dec 2025). [H]

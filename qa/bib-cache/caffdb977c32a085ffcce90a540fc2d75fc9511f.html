SOCIAL CONTRIBUTION

Establishment of Soulbrain Nanum (Sharing) Foundation

Soulbrain recognizes the importance of not only continuous corporate development, but also social responsibility, and therefore established the Soulbrain Nanum (Sharing) Foundation.

Going forward, through return of corporate profit to the constituent members of society in all walks of life, we will take a long-term viewpoint and implement a systematic plan of action to play a part in building a society that works together for everyone.

We are a Swiss company with global markets

Comet with annual sales of CHF 457,0 million (2025), is Headquartered in Flamatt, Switzerland, and has a presence in all world markets.

We employ more than 1,800 people worldwide, including about 700 in Switzerland. Besides production facilities in China, Denmark, Germany, Malaysia, Switzerland and the USA, we maintain various other subsidiaries in Canada, China, Japan, Korea, Taiwan and the USA.

Comet (COTN) is listed on the SIX Swiss Exchange.

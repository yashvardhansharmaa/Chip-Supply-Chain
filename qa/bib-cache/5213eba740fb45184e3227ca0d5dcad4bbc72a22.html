Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs) about Transformers - Hitachi Energy's reliable and sustainable solutions

Find quick answers to common questions about transformers, their role in power systems, and how Hitachi Energy solutions deliver efficiency and reliability.

Transformers are key components of our electric power infrastructure. They transfer electric energy from one alternating current to one or more other circuits, either increasing or reducing the voltage.

Hitachi Energy transformers serve various markets, including data centers, marine, railways, solar power, wind energy, utilities, nuclear power, industrial, and hydrogen.

Transformers can be liquid-filled or dry-type. They transfer electric energy from one alternating current to one or more other circuits, either increasing or reducing the voltage.

Transformers allow utilities, industries, and infrastructure sectors to maximize return on transformer assets by ensuring high reliability and optimized performance while lowering lifecycle costs and environmental impact.

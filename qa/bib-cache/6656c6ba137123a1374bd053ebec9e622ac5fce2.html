About InnoLight


InnoLight was founded in 2008, is now a world leader in development of optical transceivers and provide a wide range of high-speed optical solutions for optical communication networking, especially for AI and Data Center applications by global top tier data center operators and cloud service providers. In addition, InnoLight also provides optical transceivers solutions for enterprise data networking applications, metro and long haul transport networking, and mobile access networking applications.


InnoLight Global Presence

    InnoLight Technology Pte. Limited

    (UEN: 201832470G) was established in 2018, and operates in Singapore as InnoLight Technology’s international headquarter, integrating sales, marketing, R&D and investment functions.

    InnoLight Technology USA INC.

    InnoLight USA, the company's origin, is dedicated to advanced R&D, marketing, customer service, and delivering innovative, high-quality products promptly, all to support the company’s business growth.

    InnoLight Technology Pte. Limited (Thailand)

    InnoLight established the Thailand high volume manufacturing plant in 2019, established R&D and engineering capabilities, supporting global customer demand with state-of-the-art capabilities with high quality and competitive cost at high volume.

    InnoLight Technology Pte. Limited (Taiwan)

    InnoLight Taiwan capitalizes on the local semiconductor and device industries to enhance its service and delivery capabilities for global customers.

    InnoLight Technology (Suzhou) Ltd

    InnoLight Suzhou serves as the company RD, engineering and production facility, supports global customers.

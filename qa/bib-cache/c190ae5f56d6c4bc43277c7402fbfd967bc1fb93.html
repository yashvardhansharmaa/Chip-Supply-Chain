JP EN

Rapidus Corporation aims to develop and manufacture the world's most advanced logic semiconductors through 2nm GAA process. Rapidus will create new industries together with our customers through the development and provision of services to shorten cycle times in design, wafer processes, 3D packaging and more. The pilot line has been in operation since April 2025 at IIM, our integrated innovation and manufacturing hub in Chitose, Hokkaido. Mass production is scheduled to begin in 2027. Rapidus will continue to challenge itself in order to contribute to the fulfillment, prosperity and happiness of people's lives through the use of semiconductors.

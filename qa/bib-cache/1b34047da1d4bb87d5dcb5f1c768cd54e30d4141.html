How We Do It

Welcome to Jabil

Our team helps the world’s leading brands design, build, and scale products across global markets. Leveraging more than 60 years of experience across nearly every industry, we solve their toughest challenges by leading with engineering, forging resilient supply chains, and delivering comprehensive manufacturing solutions.

WHAT WE DO

Tailored solutions across the product lifecycle accelerate your time-to-market

Design & Engineering
Our 9,000+ engineers partner with customers from prototype to final production, engaging early in product development to ensure designs are functional, manufacturable, and market-ready.
Explore
Supply Chain Management
Backed by our $30 billion annual purchasing spend and global network of 38,000-plus suppliers, we help accelerate product development by mitigating risk in our customers’ supply chain by leveraging proven expertise, processes, and technology.
explore
Manufacturing
Jabil’s manufacturing models are personalized to each customer’s specific production needs, all led by our focus on consistently delivering the highest quality products.
Explore
Post-Production
We help our customers maximize the valuable life of their products and electronic components through return, refurbishment, and repair services, reducing their environmental impact and improving compliance with shifting regulations.

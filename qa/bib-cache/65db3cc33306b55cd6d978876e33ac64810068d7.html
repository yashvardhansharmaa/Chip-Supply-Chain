Testing the World’s Most Demanding Semiconductors

Global Provider of Advanced Semiconductor Test and Inspection and Metrology Solutions

Cohu® was founded in 1947 and is a global technology leader supplying test, automation, inspection and metrology products, software analytics solutions and services to the semiconductor industry. Cohu’s differentiated and broad product portfolio enables optimized yield and productivity, accelerating customers’ manufacturing time-to-market. Cohu solutions support critical semiconductor applications including artificial intelligence, high-performance computing, automotive and autonomous systems, advanced medical equipment, and global connectivity.

Accelerating Time to Yield and Greater Productivity

Test and handling equipment, thermal subsystems, test contacting, vision inspection & metrology and MEMS test solutions achieving higher yield faster. “Time to Yield” is a key benefit to our customers.

Cohu is a leading supplier of semiconductor test automation and interface solutions, and a growing provider of semiconductor test equipment, inspection and metrology solutions, and software analytics to optimize semiconductor manufacturing yield and productivity.

Test Handlers

Portfolio of equipment used to automate the movement of semiconductor devices, control temperature during test and provide vision inspection; ensures quality and optimizes yield in volume production.

Explore our Products

Semiconductor Testers

Portfolio of instrumentation and universal Automatic Test Equipment (ATE) platform for testing semiconductor devices with best-in-class solutions in RF, digital and mixed signal applications, enables faster time to yield.

Explore our Products

Interface Solutions

Full suite of test contactors that provide the electrical interface between the tester and the semiconductor device presented by the test handler; optimizes signal performance via an array of consumable probe pins.

Explore our Products

Inspection and Metrology

Portfolio of test and back-end finishing equipment used to provide advanced quality inspection; ensures quality and optimizes yield in volume production.

Quick Guide to JX Advanced Metals

The Businesses of JX Advanced Metals

JX Advanced Metals is a global player in advanced materials related to copper, minor metals, and other nonferrous metals. We operate our businesses based on an integrated supply chain that extends from securing resources to recycling.

Strategic Business Portfolio for Society in 2040

Social trends surrounding the JX Advanced Metals Group’s business environment are undergoing major changes, including the advance of the data society and the growing global momentum toward achieving the SDGs.
Against this backdrop, the Group has formulated its Long-Term Vision 2040, aiming to transform JX Advanced Metals Group into a technology-based company.
We seek to achieve sustainable growth by positioning each business as either a Focus Business, at the core of our growth strategy, or a Base Business, supporting our organizational foundation.

Green Hybrid Smelting Combining Copper Ore and Scrap

Green Hybrid Smelting

  • In the smelting process, recycled raw materials are melted using the heat generated by chemical reactions from copper concentrate itself, making the use of fossil fuels almost unnecessary.
  • As we increase the use of recycled raw materials, copper concentrate continues to be used as a heat source, maintaining an optimal balance between the two.

Green Hybrid Smelting was selected as one of 26 particularly noteworthy examples of Japan’s circular economy initiatives in the Noteworthy Cases Collection 2022 issued by the Circular Economy Partnership, which was established by the Ministry of the Environment; the Ministry of Economy, Trade and Industry; and Keidanren.

Initiatives to Ensure Long-Term Stable Procurement of Minor Metal Resources

To strengthen our supply chain, we are actively promoting recycling while also investing in minor metal mines and exploring new development projects.

Mibra Mine, Brazil

To secure raw materials for high-purity tantalum powder and tantalum sputtering targets, we have participated since January 2023 in a tantalum raw material production business at the Mibra Mine in Brazil, operated by AMG Brasil.

Australian Mineral Sands Project

In 2025, we concluded an agreement to acquire an interest in a mineral sands deposit development project in Australia by RZ Resources Limited, known as the Copi Project. Feasibility studies and other activities are currently underway for the project, with the aim of securing a diverse range of minerals, including minor metals and rare earths.

The History of JX Advanced Metals

Since our founding in 1905, the Group has worked on creating new value, even while responding to various changes in the business environment. We are determined to continue our challenge, contributing to the development of a sustainable economy and society through creation and innovation in the areas of resources and materials.

1905Established

Start of operation at the Hitachi Mine

Founder Fusanosuke Kuhara started operation at the Hitachi Mine, launching the Mineral resources development business and the smelting and refining business. From the beginning, Kuhara sought to boost productivity by actively embracing mechanization and modernization. The Hitachi Mine was a pioneer in development as a full-fledged custom smelter that processed not only its own ores, but also ores purchased from other companies.

1914

Construction of the Hitachi Giant Stack

The rapid growth of the Hitachi Mine's business led to a severe smoke pollution problem in the surrounding area. To deal with the pollution, the Company built what was then the world's tallest stack at 155.7 meters. It began operation in March of the following year.

1916

Start of operation at the Saganoseki Smelter & Refinery

In order to further expand the mining, smelting and refining segments fundamental to the Company's diversified business, the Saganoseki Smelter & Refinery was built in Oita Prefecture as one of the largest such plants in Japan. It remains even to this day an important center of the JX Advanced Metals Corporation Group, as a leadingedge smelter boasting the world's top levels of technology and production capacity.

1964

Kurami Works launched

With the startup of the Kurami Works (Kanagawa Prefecture), the Company made a strong entry into the metal fabrication business. Introducing the latest rolling mills, it produced phosphor bronze and other rolled copper products. By answering the needs of complex markets for flexible manufacturing of diverse products in small lots and production-to-order, while meeting demands for advanced product technology, the Company established a solid position in the metal fabrication field.

1978

New recycling furnace built

A new recycling furnace making use of smelting techniques was built at the Hitachi Smelter & Refinery, marking the start of the recycling and environmental services business. The Company pursued recovery of valuable metals from plating sludge and other industrial waste as a business.

1985

Isohara Works launched

The Company got into the electronic materials business in time for the rise of the electronics industry in the 1980s, making sputtering targets for semiconductors and transparent conductive films for liquid crystal displays, as well as materials for compound semiconductors. By establishing the Isohara Works (Ibaraki Prefecture) as a new main manufacturing center,
the Company expanded into development and manufacturing of copper foil and various electronics materials.

2014

Caserones Copper Mine began production

The official opening of the Caserones Copper Mine was calebrated, and the shipment of copper concentrate was begun.

2018

H.C. Starck Tantalum and Niobium GmbH (present TANIOBIS GmbH) shares acquired

The outstanding shares of H.C. Starck Tantalum and Niobium GmbH (present TANIOBIS GmbH), a German manufacturer of metal powders, were acquired with an eye to expanding business areas, in anticipation of dramatic demand growth for electronic components and devices.

2025

Listed on the Tokyo Stock Exchange Prime Market

JX Advanced Metals Corporation was newly listed on the Prime Market of the Tokyo Stock Exchange on March 19, 2025. Through the listing, the Company aims to enhance corporate value by establishing a management structure that enables highly specialized and agile decision-making, achieving an optimal capital structure suited to the characteristics of its businesses, and accelerating capital investment and R&D in highly competitive fields such as semiconductor materials and information and communications materials.

2026

Opening of the Hitachinaka Factory

We aim to enhance our competitiveness in the global semiconductor supply chain by strengthening our production and supply capabilities for advanced materials, including sputtering targets for semiconductors, while also advancing research and development and creating new businesses.

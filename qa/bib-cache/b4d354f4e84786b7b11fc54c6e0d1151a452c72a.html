Semiconductor

Interweaving Analysis and Measurement for a Sustainable Tomorrow

Semiconductors and Materials do not exist as distinct markets, but are interconnected, forming a value chain similar to that of textiles. The beginning of this process is Wafer. Each process, like Deposition, Lithography, Etching, and CMP, has a very large value chain. Moreover, the process is supported by huge facilities.
This interconnected system is the fabric of the value chain, or “Woven Value." HORIBA's competencies in Control, Measurement and Analysis are enabling researchers, developers, original equipment manufacturers, and end users to deliver novel solutions to the entire value chain.

We provide integrated solutions to the value chains from Semiconductor manufacturing process control and material analysis to facility monitoring and management.

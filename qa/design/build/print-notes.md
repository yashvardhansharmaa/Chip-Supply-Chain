## Changed from the report, and why
- **Fonts (P2-3, P2-4, P2-9).** The cover's 400 at optical size 144 and the wordmark's 500 at 28 are loaded under names of their own ("Fraunces Display", "Fraunces Mark") through the FontFace API, from the same Google Fonts requests. Under the family's own name, a second 400 or 500 would replace the one already loaded (one face to a weight) and change the card's figures and every head. `pdffonts`: all CID TrueType, no Type 3 (book, chapters, summary); `Fraunces96pt-Bold` is gone, `Fraunces144pt-Regular` and `Fraunces28pt-Medium` are new. `pdftotext` gives whole words.
- **P1-3 lede.** The flashcard clause changed for P3-2, and the counts differ by chapter, so no number: "…who controls the chain. Each chapter ends with questions to answer from memory. Online, every chapter also has a 3D model, and most have an animation." "Online" in place of "On the site". The rest is taken from the screen's sentence ("This page covers" becomes "This edition covers"), so an edit there reaches paper.
- **P1-4 card.** Built on floats(), as asked: the card moves past whole paragraphs only, and whenever the paragraph fits (a figure still waits for a page under 80 percent). The fit is measured on the page and is sometimes off by a few millimetres, so finish() reports a move whose paragraph lands above the card on the next page (`failed`); pdf.py undoes it and never retries it. pdf.py's score counts a card that could still move, or a failed one twice, after page count and before empty room, so the plate layout wins a tie. A card that can move is not also set closer (`tight`). pdf.py lays out up to six times (was four): the book needs the extra passes.
- **P2-1.** The stage numbers are in `--muted` (AA), not `#8b9198`.
- **P2-2.** Paged.js keeps one selector list per string name, so the report's `.rv-title { string-set: none }` does nothing. The heads come from `.prose > h3`, which leaves out the deck's hidden "Flashcards" heading. `@top-right` carries "Chip Supply Chain" before `string(section)`: Chrome's own print drops the string() and keeps the name (checked).
- **P3-2.** Each question and takeaway is kept whole (`break-inside: avoid`): in the 16-chapter trial Paged.js split a takeaway across pages 81 and 82 and lost its first line ("TEXT OFF THE PAGE").
- **P1-5.** Also catches a chapter PDF whose citation stands alone on its last page; the closer list rarely pulls it back (design-and-eda, component-costs), and the layout is kept only if better.
- **Summary.** The screen page's band tag says "Moderately hard to substitute" too (same file, same fault). `td.t` is `nowrap` with the 4 mm padding: with the wider margins two rows wrapped and the across-the-chain section fell to page 2, five pages.
- `chapter_pdfs.py` passes `--port` to pdf.py; `cards.py` takes `--out <dir>` for trials.

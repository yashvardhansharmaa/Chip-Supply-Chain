# Brand and marketing review

Options only. Read against `dist/index.html` built 10 Sept 2026, 15:08; shots in `qa/brand/`. Another session rewrote the third door mid-review. Alternatives fit the current length.

## Verdict

1. The hero lands. Five words and ten, plain and honest about scope.
2. The doors read well, but their eyebrows mix minutes with chapters, so cost cannot be compared.
3. Chokepoint is the site's name, used six times on home, defined nowhere.
4. Ratings read as measurements. Substitutability is the site's own "most opinionated field".
5. Sharing is weakest. Bare title, long description, no Open Graph or Twitter card.

## 6. What it should not claim

It is an explainer, and the outreach should not dress it as research, a dataset or a measurement. Six of the seventeen matrix rows with a basis are the site's own estimate, and the Taiwan row's note calls it "not a published measurement". Concentration and substitutability are readings, not results. The source policy narrows deliberately, so it is not comprehensive; it carries a build date, so it is a snapshot, not a tracker; and it describes leverage without recommending policy.

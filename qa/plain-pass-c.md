### ASIC

**why, after:** The five largest cloud companies spent $448 billion of capex in 2025 and have told investors to expect more in 2026, and that is the number every firm in this chain plans against.

### chiplet
**plain, before:** One piece of a chip that has been deliberately split into several smaller pieces, each manufactured separately and then wired back together inside a single package. It is a house built from prefabricated rooms rather than poured as one slab.

**plain, after:** One of several smaller pieces that a chip has been deliberately split into, each manufactured separately and then wired back together inside a single package. A chip built this way is a house assembled from prefabricated rooms rather than poured as one slab.

### compute threshold
**plain, before:** A line drawn at the arithmetic used to train a model rather than at the hardware that trains it. The 2023 US executive order on AI set its reporting line at "any model that was trained using a quantity of computing power greater than 10^26 integer or floating-point operations" (Executive Order 14110, section 4.2(b)(i), 88 FR 75191). The same number became an export control in January 2025: ECCN 4E091 covers "'Parameters' for an artificial intelligence model trained utilizing 10^26 or more 'operations'", and 15 CFR 742.6(a)(13) requires a licence for them to every destination worldwide. Europe drew its line an order of magnitude lower, at a cumulative training run "greater than 10^25" floating point operations, in Article 51(2) of the EU AI Act.

**plain, after:** A limit set on the amount of arithmetic used to train an AI model, rather than on the chips that do the arithmetic. The 2023 US executive order on AI set its reporting line at "any model that was trained using a quantity of computing power greater than 10^26 integer or floating-point operations" (Executive Order 14110, section 4.2(b)(i), 88 FR 75191). The same number became an export control in January 2025: ECCN 4E091 covers "'Parameters' for an artificial intelligence model trained utilizing 10^26 or more 'operations'", and 15 CFR 742.6(a)(13) requires a licence for them to every destination worldwide. Europe drew its line ten times lower, at a cumulative training run "greater than 10^25" floating point operations, in Article 51(2) of the EU AI Act.

### Concentration
**plain, before:** A four-band judgement of how many firms could credibly supply a stage today, taken at the narrowest step inside it. Extreme means three or fewer, or one firm holding more than half the stage. High means four to six, with the top two taking most of the revenue. Moderate means seven or more, none of them dominant. Low means many, with the scarce input something another industry sells at volume. Where the country-share matrix carries a figure for the segment, the top country's share is a second check, and the supplier count wins when the two disagree.

**plain, after:** A four-band judgement of how many firms could credibly supply a stage today, taken at the narrowest step inside it. Extreme means three or fewer, or one firm holding more than half the stage. High means four to six, with the top two taking most of the revenue. Moderate means seven or more, none of them dominant. Low means many, with the scarce input something another industry sells at volume. Where a figure exists for the top country's share of the segment, it serves as a second check, and the supplier count wins when the two disagree.

### Country Groups
**plain, before:** The buckets US export rules sort the world into, published as supplement no. 1 to 15 CFR part 740. A:5 and A:6 are close partners; D:1, D:4 and D:5 are the national security, missile technology and arms-embargoed groups; E:1 and E:2 are the most restricted. A country can sit in several at once, and the licence requirement usually turns on which.

**plain, after:** The groups that US export rules sort the world's countries into, published as supplement no. 1 to 15 CFR part 740. A:5 and A:6 are close partners; D:1, D:4 and D:5 are the national security, missile technology and arms-embargoed groups; E:1 and E:2 are the most restricted. A country can sit in several at once, and the licence requirement usually turns on which.

### de minimis
**plain, before:** The share of controlled US content a foreign-made item may carry before it becomes subject to the EAR. Under 15 CFR 734.4 the general threshold is 25 percent of the item's value, and 10 percent for the most restricted destinations. Above the line the item is caught; below it, it is not.

**plain, after:** The share of controlled American content a foreign-made item may contain before US export rules, the EAR, apply to it. Under 15 CFR 734.4 the general threshold is 25 percent of the item's value, and 10 percent for the most restricted destinations. Above the line the item is caught; below it, it is not.

### denial order
**plain, before:** An enforcement order, not a licence policy. Under 15 CFR 764.3 it suspends or revokes a named party's outstanding licences and bars exports, reexports and in-country transfers of any item subject to the EAR to or by that party. There is no application to file. Orders can be issued outright or suspended and later activated.

**plain, after:** An order from BIS that shuts a named party out of trade in US-controlled items as a penalty, rather than a policy for reviewing licences. Under 15 CFR 764.3 it suspends or revokes the party's outstanding licences and bars exports, reexports and in-country transfers of any item subject to the EAR to or by that party. There is no application to file. Orders can be issued outright or suspended and later activated.

### EAR
**plain, before:** The body of US rules that governs the export of commercial and dual-use goods, software and technology, written and enforced by the Bureau of Industry and Security. They run from 15 CFR part 730 to part 774: part 734 sets what they cover, part 742 the licence requirements, part 744 the end-use and end-user controls, part 740 the exceptions and part 764 the penalties.

**plain, after:** The body of US rules that governs the export of commercial goods, software and technology, including items with both civilian and military uses, written and enforced by the Bureau of Industry and Security. They run from 15 CFR part 730 to part 774: part 734 sets what they cover, part 742 the licence requirements, part 744 the end-use and end-user controls, part 740 the exceptions and part 764 the penalties.

### EAR99
**plain, before:** The label for an item that is subject to the EAR but is not listed anywhere on the Commerce Control List, from 15 CFR 734.3(c). It is a residual category, not a free pass: an EAR99 item still needs a licence if it is going to a listed party or a controlled end use.

**plain, after:** The label for an item that falls under US export rules but is not named on the Commerce Control List, the catalogue of controlled items, from 15 CFR 734.3(c). It is a residual category, not a free pass: an EAR99 item still needs a licence if it is going to a listed party or a controlled end use.

### Entity List
**plain, before:** A published list of foreign companies, institutes and individuals to whom any exporter of an item subject to the EAR needs a licence, not only American suppliers. Each entry states its own licence review policy, so a listing is not one uniform outcome: most carry a presumption of denial; SMIC's is case by case except for items uniquely capable of production at 10 nanometres and below. It is supplement no. 4 to 15 CFR part 744, and the licence requirement is in 15 CFR 744.11.

**plain, after:** A published list of foreign companies, institutes and individuals that need a licence before anyone, not only an American supplier, may send them an item subject to the EAR. Each entry states its own licence review policy, so a listing is not one uniform outcome: most carry a presumption of denial, meaning the licence will normally be refused; SMIC's is case by case except for items uniquely capable of production at 10 nanometres and below. It is supplement no. 4 to 15 CFR part 744, and the licence requirement is in 15 CFR 744.11.

### fabless
**plain, before:** A chip company that designs and sells chips but owns no factory, paying a foundry to manufacture them. Nvidia, AMD, Apple, Qualcomm and Broadcom all work this way.

**plain, after:** A chip company that designs and sells chips but owns no factory, paying a contract manufacturer, a foundry, to make them. Nvidia, AMD, Apple, Qualcomm and Broadcom all work this way.

### FinFET
**plain, before:** A transistor whose current-carrying channel is stood on edge like a shark fin so that the controlling gate wraps around three of its sides instead of resting on one. More contact means firmer control and less leakage when the switch is meant to be off.

**plain, after:** A transistor whose channel, the strip that current flows through, is stood on edge like a shark fin, so that the gate, the electrode that switches the current, wraps around three of its sides instead of resting on one. More contact means firmer control and less leakage when the switch is meant to be off.

### foreign direct product rule
**plain, before:** A US rule that pulls a foreign-made item under American control when two tests are both met: a product scope, meaning the item is the direct product of specified US technology or software, or was made by a plant that is; and a destination or end-user scope, meaning it is going somewhere or to someone the rule names. Both halves are needed. The mechanics are in 15 CFR 734.9.

**plain, after:** A US rule that puts a foreign-made item under American control when two tests are both met. The first is about the product: the item was made directly from specified US technology or software, or in a plant that was. The second is about the destination: the item is going somewhere, or to someone, the rule names. Both halves are needed. The mechanics are in 15 CFR 734.9.

### FP8 and FP4
**plain, before:** Ways of writing a number using only 8 or 4 bits instead of the 32 a computer traditionally uses. Each number is less precise, but the chip can perform and store far more of them, which suits machine learning better than it would suit accounting.

**plain, after:** Ways of writing a number using only 8 or 4 binary digits, or bits, instead of the 32 a computer traditionally uses. Each number is less precise, but the chip can perform and store far more of them, which suits machine learning better than it would suit accounting.

### gate-all-around
**plain, before:** A transistor in which the controlling gate surrounds the channel that current flows through, rather than touching three sides as a fin does. Surrounding it gives the gate total authority, so the switch still turns fully off as dimensions shrink.

**plain, after:** A transistor in which the gate, the electrode that switches the current, wraps all the way round the channel that current flows through, rather than touching three sides as a fin does. Surrounding the channel gives the gate full control, so the switch still turns fully off as dimensions shrink.

### High-NA EUV
**plain, before:** The next generation of extreme ultraviolet machine, whose larger mirrors collect light at a wider angle and so resolve smaller features. The wider optics also halve the area printable in one exposure, so a large chip has to be stitched together from two of them.

**plain, after:** The next generation of extreme ultraviolet machine, whose larger mirrors collect light over a wider angle and so print smaller features. The wider optics also halve the area the machine can print in one exposure, so a large chip has to be stitched together from two exposures.

### hybrid bonding
**plain, before:** Joining two chips face to face by pressing their copper pads and the surrounding oxide into direct contact, with no solder in between. Removing the solder lets connections be packed much more tightly and shortens the path heat has to travel out.

**plain, after:** Joining two chips face to face by pressing their copper pads, and the glass-like insulator around them, into direct contact, with no solder in between. Removing the solder lets connections be packed much more tightly and shortens the path heat has to travel out.

### in-country transfer
**why, before:** It is the leg that catches diversion after landing, and it is why a licence granted for a named Chinese customer does not travel with the hardware. Reexports and in-country transfers of advanced chips to Country Group D:5 keep a presumption of denial even where an export would be read case by case (91 FR 1685).

**why, after:** It is the step that catches hardware diverted after it has landed, and it is why a licence granted for a named Chinese customer does not travel with the hardware. Reexports and in-country transfers of advanced chips to Country Group D:5 keep a presumption of denial even where an export would be read case by case (91 FR 1685).

### known-good die
**plain, before:** A bare chip tested thoroughly enough, before assembly, to be trusted inside something expensive. In an ordinary product a marginal chip costs one chip; inside a stacked package it can destroy thirty.

**plain, after:** A bare chip tested thoroughly enough, before assembly, to be trusted inside something expensive. In an ordinary product a faulty chip wastes one chip; inside a stacked package it can ruin thirty.

**why, before:** As packages grow toward ten logic chips and twenty memory stacks, the cost of catching a bad one late pushes testing and burn-in back onto the wafer.

**why, after:** As packages grow toward ten logic chips and twenty memory stacks, the cost of catching a bad one late pushes testing and burn-in, running chips hot to shake out early failures, back onto the wafer.

### License Exception ACA
**plain, before:** The companion to NAC in 15 CFR 740.8, added in December 2024. It authorises the same classes of advanced computing chips worldwide except to Macau and Country Group D:5 entities, without a notification, and allows some transfers within those destinations. Like NAC, it does not reach the data centre parts that meet the 3A090.a parameters, which need a licence to any destination worldwide.

**plain, after:** A carve-out in 15 CFR 740.8, added in December 2024, that lets the same classes of advanced computing chips as its companion exception NAC ship worldwide except to Macau and Country Group D:5 entities, with no advance notification, and allows some transfers within those destinations. Like NAC, it does not reach the data centre parts that meet the 3A090.a parameters, which need a licence to any destination worldwide.

### License Exception HBM
**plain, before:** The exception at 15 CFR 740.25 that carries ECCN 3A090.c memory into the one place a licence is needed. The Commerce Control List applies the regional stability control on 3A090.c only "to or within Macau or a destination specified in Country Group D:5", so a stack sold to an allied packaging house needs no licence at any density. Inside China, 740.25(c) authorises co-packaging only below a memory bandwidth density of 3.3 GB/s per square millimetre.

**plain, after:** A carve-out at 15 CFR 740.25 that lets ECCN 3A090.c memory, high bandwidth memory, into the one region where a licence is otherwise needed. The Commerce Control List applies the regional stability control on 3A090.c only "to or within Macau or a destination specified in Country Group D:5", so a stack sold to an allied packaging house needs no licence at any density. Inside China, 740.25(c) authorises co-packaging, putting the memory into a package with a chip, only below a memory bandwidth density of 3.3 GB/s per square millimetre.

### License Exception LPP
**plain, before:** The exception at 15 CFR 740.29, created by the AI Diffusion Rule of January 2025 (90 FR 4544). It authorises up to 26,900,000 cumulative total processing performance of advanced computing chips a year to one ultimate consignee, to any destination except Macau, Country Group D:5 and consignees headquartered in either. It does not authorise transfers within a country.

**plain, after:** The exception at 15 CFR 740.29, created by the AI Diffusion Rule of January 2025 (90 FR 4544). It authorises advanced computing chips adding up to 26,900,000 cumulative total processing performance, the export rules' measure of computing power, a year to one ultimate consignee, the final recipient, to any destination except Macau, Country Group D:5 and consignees headquartered in either. It does not authorise transfers within a country.

### performance density
**plain, before:** A chip's TPP divided by the area of its logic dies in square millimetres, memory stacks excluded. It applies only to a chip that already reaches 1,600 TPP, and it catches parts that spread the same computing power over more silicon. It is the test that replaced the 2022 interconnect-bandwidth criterion.

**plain, after:** A chip's TPP, the export rules' measure of computing power, divided by the area of its logic dies in square millimetres, memory stacks excluded. It applies only to a chip that already reaches 1,600 TPP, and it catches parts that spread the same computing power over more silicon. It replaced the 2022 test, which was based on interconnect bandwidth, the speed at which a chip exchanges data with its neighbours.

### reexport
**plain, before:** Shipping an item that is subject to the EAR from one foreign country to another, defined at 15 CFR 734.14. A Korean stack of memory moving from Korea to China is a reexport, and it needs the same US licence a shipment from Texas would.

**plain, after:** Shipping an item that falls under US export rules, the EAR, from one foreign country to another, defined at 15 CFR 734.14. A Korean stack of memory moving from Korea to China is a reexport, and it needs the same US licence a shipment from Texas would.

### Substitutability
**plain, before:** A four-band rating of how hard it would be for a well-funded state or firm, starting today, to stand up an alternative to the leaders of a stage, judged against four written tests. Easy: a qualified second source ships today at the leading node. Moderate: a second source exists but is not yet qualified at the leading node, about two to five years to close the gap. Hard: no second source at the leading node, five to ten years, and the path is known. Very hard: no second source, and the path runs through physics, decades of unwritten know-how or protected IP, ten years or more, and nobody has repeated it yet. The clock always times the same actor and runs to qualification, not to a prototype.

**plain, after:** A four-band rating of how hard it would be for a well-funded state or firm, starting today, to build an alternative to the leaders of a stage, judged against four written tests. Easy: a second supplier is qualified, meaning approved by customers, and shipping today at the leading node, the most advanced process. Moderate: a second supplier exists but is not yet qualified at the leading node, about two to five years to close the gap. Hard: no second supplier at the leading node, five to ten years, and the path is known. Very hard: no second supplier, and the path runs through physics, decades of unwritten know-how or protected intellectual property, ten years or more, and nobody has repeated it yet. The clock always times the same actor and runs to qualification, not to a prototype.

### TPP
**plain, before:** The measure US export rules use for a chip's computing power: twice the number of multiply-accumulate operations it does each second, multiplied by the number of bits in each operation, added up across every processing unit on the chip. The definition sits in ECCN 3A090, in supplement no. 1 to 15 CFR part 774.

**plain, after:** The measure US export rules use for a chip's computing power: twice the number of multiply-accumulate operations, a multiply followed by an add, that it does each second, multiplied by the number of bits in each operation, added up across every processing unit on the chip. The definition sits in ECCN 3A090, in supplement no. 1 to 15 CFR part 774.

**why, before:** The dividing line for sales to China. Control starts at 4,800; since 15 January 2026 applications below 21,000 TPP and 6,500 GB/s of memory bandwidth are read case by case (91 FR 1685). Accelerators are now designed around the formula.

**why, after:** The dividing line for sales to China. Control starts at 4,800 TPP; since 15 January 2026 applications below 21,000 TPP and 6,500 GB/s of memory bandwidth are read case by case (91 FR 1685). Accelerators are now designed around the formula.

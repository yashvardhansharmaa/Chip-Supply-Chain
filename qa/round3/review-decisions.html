1. Large groups: whole-group revenue or the chip-related segment

Eight rows are businesses inside large groups: Merck KGaA, Linde, Air Liquide, Schneider Electric, Eaton, Siemens Energy, Infineon and Wacker Chemie. The old figures were whole-group totals, and the new ones are too, so a reader sees Merck at its whole group's €21,102 million in 2025, while its electronics business, the part in the chip supply chain, had €3,515 million. Recommendation: keep the whole-group figure, as filed, and label these eight rows "group revenue" so no one reads them as chip revenue.

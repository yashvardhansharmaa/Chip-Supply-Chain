# Jargon audit: glossary coverage

Scope: the prose of chapters 01 to 17 (the body under the headings, not the front matter, the "In plain terms" box or the "In short" section), the 92 entries in `data/glossary.json`, and the interface strings a reader meets on the Suppliers, AI Chips and Export Controls pages (`src/template.html`, the `Landscape`, `Suppliers` and `Geo` renderers in `src/app.js`, `data/control-flows.json`, `data/landscape.json`, `data/suppliers.json`). Nothing in the site was edited. Date: 14 September 2026.

## Counts

- Audit rows: **256** terms or term groups examined (tables below): 197 with no entry, 9 with an entry that covers the idea but not the form used, and 50 with an entry, most of which the matcher misses at the text's first occurrence. Of the 256, 81 are marked "skip" because the chapter explains the term where it stands, or it appears once, or it is absent from the prose.
- Glossary entries proposed: **109** (JSON array, ready to append).
- Existing entries needing an `aka` form added or corrected: **56** (list at the end), including the 2 entries whose `aka` is silently dropped (FLOPS, Concentration) and the 1 whose form misfires (die, via `chip`).
- Interface strings with no popover at all: the AI Chips table and export-status text, the Suppliers categories and roles, and every Export Controls `what` summary. The runtime `GLOSS` list covers five forms inside state labels only.

## How the matcher works (from `build/build.js` and `src/app.js`)

- `glossaryWrap` builds one regex from every `term` and every comma-separated `aka` form. It is case-insensitive. A boundary is "not a letter or digit", so a hyphen or slash counts as a boundary but a trailing "s" does not: `wafer` does not match `wafers`, `chiplet` does not match `chiplets`, `TSV` does not match `TSVs`. Plurals match only when listed in `aka`.
- Spaces inside a form match any run of whitespace but not a hyphen: `high bandwidth memory` does not match `high-bandwidth memory`, which is how every chapter writes it.
- An `aka` field containing a semicolon is dropped whole. Any form starting "part of", "singular of", "when ", "for example" or "also " is dropped. Two entries lose their entire `aka` this way: **FLOPS** ("floating point operations per second; a TFLOPS is a trillion of them") and **Concentration** ("concentration band; Extreme, High, Moderate, Low"). So `TFLOPS` and `floating point operations per second` are never wrapped.
- Only the first occurrence of each term in a chapter is wrapped. If that first occurrence is a form the matcher does not know, the term stays unwrapped until a later known form appears, or never.
- Text inside `<a>`, `<code>`, `<sup>`, headings, `<dt>`, `<dd>`, figures and buttons is skipped. Link text is never wrapped, and a term that appears only in a heading is never wrapped.
- `die` carries `aka: chip`, so the first "chip" in every chapter's prose is wrapped as `die`. That is probably not intended in chapters where "chip" means the finished product (01, 09, 14, 16, 17).
- The runtime list `GLOSS` in `src/app.js` wraps only `TPP`, `Entity List`, `FDPR`, `foreign direct product rule` and `license exception`, and only inside Export Controls state labels. The `what` summaries under "Show full summaries", the AI Chips table and export-status paragraphs, and the Suppliers categories and roles get no popovers.

## Notes for the author

- The two semicolon `aka` fields (FLOPS, Concentration) are the only entries whose alternates are silently discarded; everything else in the list is a missed form.
- `die` with `aka: chip` is the one existing form that misfires. Chapters 01, 09, 14, 16 and 17 open with "chip" meaning the finished product, and the popover says "die".
- The proposed entries avoid bare forms that are also English words (`immersion`, `gate`, `via`, `blank`, `core`, `track`, `channel`). Where a form is safe on this site but risky in general (`lots`, `bump`, `sort`, `ramp`), it is listed and the author may cut it.
- The Suppliers chokepoint levels (Critical, Important, Contested, Diversified) have no definition anywhere on the site and are on a different scale from the chapter cards' Concentration bands. A legend or an entry would close that gap; it is a data-page decision rather than a glossary one, so no entry is proposed.
- Nothing on the AI Chips page is wrapped, including terms with entries. If the author wants popovers there, the `Landscape` renderer would need to call `gloss()` on the export-status text and row labels, with the `GLOSS` list extended as suggested in the interface table.

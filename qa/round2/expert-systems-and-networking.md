## Open questions for the owner
1. **`plain`: "draws more than a thousand amps, several times the current a whole house is wired to carry"** is uncited. The physics is right for a 1,000 to 1,400 W GPU at under 1 V. No allowed source I found states a GPU's current; vendor pages only describe regulators rated above 1,000 A. Keep, or cut the clause (it changes `plain` and its twin).
2. **Specimen caption "about 510 by 400 mm and drawn to scale"** is the designer's estimate from a ServeTheHome photo (trade press, `qa/specimen-review-systems.md`). The same issue was raised for ch 09. Drop the dimensions, or keep them.
3. **Fabrinet** (Thailand) is missing. Nvidia's 10-K names it with Hon Hai and Wistron among its contract manufacturers, and Nvidia's CPO release lists it among pluggable-transceiver leaders. The card's TH note ("Chinese module makers' offshore transceiver plants") leaves it out. Add one sourced sentence and widen the TH note?
4. **Country notes.** JP lists only "Low-loss board laminate". Japanese firms also make InP wafers (Sumitomo, JX) and lasers. CN could add circuit boards (TTM). These notes have no source fields, so I left them.
5. **"72 to a rack"** (`oneLiner`, `plain`) describes Nvidia's and AMD's design. Google's cube holds 64 (the body says so). That seems fine as a simplification; flagged only.

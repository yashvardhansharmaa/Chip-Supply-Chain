### data/glossary.json (terms with chapter photomasks-and-pellicles)
1. "mask blank" `plain`. Before: "... It is a six-inch square of very flat glass carrying the coating that the pattern will be cut into. For extreme ultraviolet the coating is a mirror of about forty pairs of ultrathin layers, and a particle buried in it cannot be repaired." After: "... It is a six-inch square of very flat glass carrying the film that the pattern will be cut into. For extreme ultraviolet that film sits on a mirror of about forty pairs of ultrathin layers, and a particle buried in the mirror cannot be repaired." Why: wrong as written. The EUV pattern is etched into the absorber on top of the mirror, and the mirror is never cut (arXiv p. 1: "Mo/Si multilayer coated by a Ru protective layer and a TaBN/TaBO absorber layer").
2. "mask blank" `why`. Before: "The Japanese glassmaker AGC says only three firms supply EUV blanks, two of them Japanese." After: "The Japanese glassmaker AGC says only three firms supply EUV blanks, and AGC and Hoya, both Japanese, are two of them." (AGC never says "two of them Japanese".) Rest unchanged.
3. "photomask" `plain`. Before: "A chip needs dozens of them, one per layer." After: "A chip needs dozens of them, at least one per layer."
4. "reticle" `why`. Before: "A reticle's printable area is fixed at 26 by 33 millimeters, and every large AI chip has to be designed around that limit." After: "One exposure from a reticle prints at most 26 by 33 millimeters of the wafer, and every large AI chip has to be designed around that limit." Why: 26 by 33 mm is the scanner's field on the wafer (ch 11, ASML NXE:3400C). The patterned area on the reticle is four times larger, and High-NA halves the field (ch 5), so "fixed" is wrong.
5. "electron beam" `plain` (minor). Before: "Fabs use it to write the pattern onto a photomask, to inspect wafers for defects light cannot see, and to measure line widths." After: "Mask shops use it to write the pattern onto a photomask, and fabs use it to inspect wafers for defects light cannot see and to measure line widths."

### Animation `mask` (same strings in `media/mask.json` steps and the `STEPS` array of `animations/mask.html`)
1. Step "One speck", `cap`. **Error, highest priority**, also printed as text on the static page. Before: "A speck of dust on the mask prints on every wafer, until someone finds it. The pellicle, a dust cover meant to protect the mask, is still short of production in a leading-edge fab." After: "A speck of dust on the mask prints on every wafer, until someone finds it. So a thin membrane on a frame, called a pellicle, sits just above the mask and catches dust where it is too far out of focus to print." Why: ASML 2022 calls the pellicle "one of the breakthroughs that enabled EUV ... lithography in high-volume microchip manufacturing", Mitsui has made EUV pellicles commercially since 2021, and the body says "Pellicles already work in high-volume EUV manufacturing". The new text copies `plain`.
2. Step "A mirror", `cap`. Before: "No glass lets the short light now in use pass through. So the mask is a mirror, built up from about forty pairs of very thin layers laid on glass, and the light bounces off it." After: "No glass lets extreme ultraviolet (EUV) light through. So the mask for EUV layers is a mirror, built up from about forty pairs of very thin layers laid on glass, and the light bounces off it." Why: "the short light now in use" is vague (deep ultraviolet light is also in use and passes through quartz), and it implies shortness is why glass blocks it, which is false (harder X-rays are shorter and pass through glass).
3. Step "The scanner", `cap`: "one for each layer of a chip" becomes "at least one for each layer of a chip".
4. `animations/mask.html` HEADS, on-canvas text, t0 5.0. Before: `h: 'Glass stops this light', s: 'The light now in use is too short to pass'`. After: `h: 'Glass stops EUV light', s: 'Every kind of glass soaks it up'` (shorter, fits). Same physics reason as 2.
5. Code comments only: `animations/mask.html` lines 9-14 ("short light now in use", "the dust cover meant to protect the mask is still not in production") and `animations/fab-loop.html` line 13 ("one for each layer"). Update to match.

## KLA's non-GAAP margin: expert view
Keep it, labelled. The June-quarter adjustment was $36.2M of acquisition-related charges in cost of revenues on $3.66B of revenue, about one point. KLA's own September guidance shows a 0.9-point gap (GAAP 60.6 to 62.6, non-GAAP 61.5 to 63.5). The table already mixes bases: US GAAP for most firms, Taiwan-IFRS for TSMC and ASE, and a segment for ASE. Their cost-of-sales definitions differ by about as much. On either basis KLA sits between TSMC and ASML, and the stated range does not move. Dropping KLA would remove the most concentrated stage in the classes table. One alternative is KLA's stated GAAP gross margin of 61.3 percent for the fiscal year to June 2026 (10-K, filed 6 Aug 2026, MD&A table). That basis matches the others but the period does not. I would not use it.

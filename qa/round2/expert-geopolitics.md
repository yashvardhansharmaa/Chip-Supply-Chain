## Newer rulings and rules that change what the chapter presented as current
- **Learning Resources, Inc. v. Trump**, US Supreme Court, No. 24-1287, decided 20 Feb 2026: "Held: IEEPA does not authorize the President to impose tariffs." The "reciprocal" tariffs (EO 14257) rested on IEEPA. Change 18.
- **FR 2026-10571** (28 May 2026): the US-Taiwan Agreement on Reciprocal Trade (signed 12 Feb 2026) "has not yet entered into force"; only the 15 Jan 2026 MOU is implemented. Change 18.
- **Section 301 actions on 60 economies** (memorandum of 23 Jul 2026, 91 FR 47717; USTR 91 FR 47318): for Taiwan, "the sum of the MFN tariff and the section 301 tariff shall be 10 percent", "consistent with their respective Agreements on Reciprocal Trade". Not added to the chapter.
- **UAE rule, 91 FR 43034**: G42's and Core42's license-free status "shall automatically expire on April 6, 2027" absent BIS notice; the preamble says they must "become U.S. companies on or before April 6, 2027". Not added; owner may want one sentence.
- **BIS guidance, 31 May 2026**: the license requirement for D:5- and Macau-headquartered entities is still enforced anywhere ("The answer is yes"). Consistent with the glossary; no change.
- **Reuters, 17 Dec 2025** (Tier B, Wayback): "Shenzhen team completed a working prototype of a EUV machine in early 2025 … has not produced working chips … targeting 2028." The chapter's dated CSIS judgment and "cannot yet copy" stay accurate; owner may want a sentence.
- **Deadlines inside four weeks**: the US Affiliates suspension ends 9 Nov 2026, MOFCOM's Notice 61 suspension 10 Nov, its gallium-ban suspension 27 Nov. eCFR shows no extension of 744.11 published as of 16 Oct.
- **Japan** widened its list from 28 May 2025 (advanced chips of 6,000 TPP or more, more tools; timeline entry). The Japan bullet stops at 2023. That is an omission, not an error.

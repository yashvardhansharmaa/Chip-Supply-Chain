## 2. Proposals for shared files (main session applies)

### `data/glossary.json`
1. **hybrid bonding**
   - `aka`: "direct bond interconnect, SoIC, Foveros, copper pad to copper pad" -> "direct bond interconnect, SoIC, Foveros Direct, copper pad to copper pad". **Important:** the build turns `aka` into popovers, and today "Foveros" opens the hybrid-bonding definition twice in ch 11: in the card's US line, and in "Intel's Foveros stacks dies face to face and joins them with solder microbumps". Plain Foveros uses solder (Intel brief: Foveros Direct is what eliminates "traditional solder microbumps").
   - `plain`: "Joining two chips face to face by pressing their copper pads, and the glass-like insulator around them, into direct contact, with no solder in between." -> "Joining two chips by bonding their copper pads, and the glass-like insulator around them, directly to each other, with no solder in between." (rest unchanged). Two errors: hybrid bonding is not only face to face (AMD V-Cache's bottom die is "placed 'face down'" and joined through TSVs, SemiAnalysis hybrid-bonding article), and the bond comes from contact and annealing, not pressing.
   - `why`: "It would cut the spacing between connections in a memory stack from about 20 micrometers to under 1." -> "An SK hynix tech note puts microbumps about 20 micrometers apart and hybrid-bonded pads under 1 micrometer apart." (second sentence unchanged, but start it "It expects full-scale use..."). The note's figures are generic, not for memory stacks; for HBM it says TCB is unsuitable "for pitches of approximately 7µm or below".
2. **microbump** `plain`: "Microbumps sit 30 to 40 microns apart, about a third of a hair's width." -> "In Intel's 2.5D packages microbumps sit 36 microns apart, about a third of a hair's width." (unsourced range; matches change 2). Optional, in `why`: "Hybrid bonding, copper pressed straight onto copper," -> "Hybrid bonding, which joins copper straight to copper,".
3. **interposer** `plain`: "...or layers of fine copper wiring with small silicon bridges." -> "...or layers of fine copper wiring, often with small silicon bridges." (CoWoS-R has none; matches `plain`). `why`: "On TSMC's CoWoS-L that area is now 5.5 times the reticle, ..." -> "In April 2026 TSMC was producing CoWoS at 5.5 times the reticle, ..." (vague "now").
4. **reticle limit** `why`: "Every leading AI accelerator needs more circuit than this, so designs are split into several dies, ..." -> "Many leading AI accelerators, Nvidia's Blackwell among them, need more circuitry than this, so they are split into several dies, ..." (the H100 is one die. The chapter itself says "Many"). Optional, at the end of `plain`: "The newer High-NA EUV machines print half that area." (ch 4 cites ASML: High-NA optics halve "the patch of wafer one exposure covers", and Intel uses High-NA in volume).
5. Optional, **thermocompression bonding** `plain`: "...and how chiplets are placed on a package." -> "...and a growing way to place chiplets on a package." (most chiplets are still attached by mass reflow; ASMPT, 4 Mar 2026: "As the industry transitions from mass reflow technology to TCB").

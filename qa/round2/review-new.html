Some of the errors the experts caught: a wire-pitch comparison between SMIC and Intel ran backwards (chapter 8); a "50 times deeper than wide" figure was a market-research analyst's quote in Lam's release, now sourced to the US rule (chapter 6); an IFP estimate credited to the United States covers all producers outside China (chapter 10); a US–Taiwan tariff sentence predated the Supreme Court's February 2026 ruling (chapter 17); three China revenue shares were off by rounding or an old segment (Camtek, Besi, Synopsys).

The wordmark

Your pick, Signature: the favicon tile beside the name in title case, on every page type and all 24 share cards.

Animations

Two new ones, critiqued and rebuilt: chapter 9's fab loop (19.6 s, ending on the finished wafer and SIA's 1,000 to 1,500 steps, as you asked) and chapter 3's materials loop. Chapter 7's defect animation now uses the textbook's own worked example, with no computed numbers.

Dates and sources on every card figure, with page numbers

Each figure on a chapter card carries a source mark that shares the chapter's footnote numbering. Citations into long PDFs now name the page ("p. 27"), and sources without a date show when they were read.

China exposure joined to the chain

Each card leader with filed China revenue shows its share and links to the firm's chart; the Suppliers page gains a China column and an EU-27 view; TSMC, Disco and Hoya join the data, now 24 firms.

Export Controls beyond the United States

Seventeen measures by other governments (the Netherlands, Japan, South Korea, Taiwan, the EU, the UK, Germany, Italy, Malaysia and others), under the category "Other governments". The lead text now says "The main rules since 2018", where it said "Every rule".

Chapter PDFs and printing

"Chapter PDF" replaces "Report an error" in each chapter's cite line; each PDF carries an edition date (20 October 2026). Printing from the browser keeps full source addresses and opens collapsed sections.

Learning aids

The Chain's introduction now mentions the 3D models, animations and flashcards; flashcards are remembered between visits and found by the site search; Start here ends with a deck of ten cards; on phones the first screen shows the whole chain at a glance.

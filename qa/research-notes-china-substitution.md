## Chinese toolmakers and lithography
- Naura FY2025 revenue RMB 39.35bn (+30.9%), net income RMB 5.52bn; AMEC FY2025 revenue RMB 12.39bn (+36.6%). (Filed figures; cite the companies' own reports rather than aggregators.)
- Domestic share of tools used in China 35% in 2025 (from 25% in 2024); localisation: etch and deposition >40%, metrology 25%, lithography 18% (includes packaging steppers). REPORTED https://www.trendforce.com/news/2026/01/12/news-chinas-domestic-chip-equipment-adoption-beats-2025-target-at-35-led-by-naura-amec/

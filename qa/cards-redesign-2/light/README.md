# Social cards, round 2: "light"

Three directions on the site's warm white (`#f7f6f2`), blue-black ink (`#14181f`) and garnet (`#7a1f2b`), in the site's own type: Fraunces for titles and numerals (SOFT and WONK at 0), Source Serif 4 for the lede, Instrument Sans for labels, Jost for the specimen line. The specimen is the chapter's own point cloud, dumped by the sibling `cards-art.js` and drawn in ink with its accent points in the colours it declares for the light ground (the EUV beam in pale blue-violet, the tin plasma violet-white, the die's copper strip), at the tilt the page opens it at. Every card carries chipsupplychain.org, uses the site's real copy, and contains no em dash. Rendered by `cards.py` here (a copy of `build/cards.py`, port 9421); the six PNGs are quantised to 256 colours and come in at 46 to 82 KB. `sheet.png` is the contact sheet.

## Plate (`plate-home.png`, `plate-lithography.png`)

The specimen is the card. It sits large at the right on the bare warm white, exactly as the page shows it: no gradient, no vignette, the centered 28 px rule and the tracked "SPECIMEN 04 · LITHOGRAPHY" line in Jost beneath it with the number in garnet. The left column is the chapter head reduced to its essentials: the eyebrow ("Chip Supply Chain | Chapter 04 · Stage 4 of 15" in tracked Instrument Sans), the title in Fraunces at 110 px, the two ratings as small garnet-ruled tags in the site's `.ptag` style, and the first two sentences of the chapter's lede in Source Serif. The home card swaps the eyebrow for the wordmark in small caps, "How AI chips are made" and the site's subtitle, with the die floorplan and "SPECIMEN 01 · CHIP DESIGN, EDA AND IP". At 300 px the title and tags read, the specimen reads as a drawing, and the card is unmistakably a page from this site.

## Ledger (`ledger-home.png`, `ledger-lithography.png`)

The card is the chapter page's own head, cropped. A hairline masthead carries the wordmark and tagline with "The Chain" at the far right. Below it the ruled ledger column from the chapter page: "CHAPTER", the light "04" in Fraunces 300 at opsz 144, "STAGE 4 OF 15", then the fifteen-row stage index under an ink rule, each row's bar at its band width (Extreme in garnet, High in ink, the rest in the hairline grey), the current row ticked in the margin with its number in garnet, and the foot that names the stage and the next one. Beside it the title, the lede, and the two ratings as tick-separated labels; the specimen sits smaller at the right over its plate line. For the home card the column reads "THE CHAIN / 15 / STAGES" with every bar drawn and no row current, and the meta line gives the site's three counts (17 chapters, 220 suppliers, 35 AI chips). The index is a real graphic: it tells a reader in one glance where the chapter falls and how concentrated the chain is around it. It costs the title about 15 percent of its size against Plate, and the stage index is texture rather than text at feed size, but the "04" and the garnet rows survive.

## Title (`title-home.png`, `title-lithography.png`)

Typography only. The title is set huge in Fraunces light (190 px for "Lithography", 162 px for the two-line home title, which keeps the site's own hero break), a single 1 px garnet hairline under it, the lede in Source Serif at 30 px beneath, and the specimen small and low at the right like a printer's mark with "SPECIMEN 04" under it. The eyebrow at the top left names the site, chapter and stage; the ratings run as one tracked line at the top right. The point cloud is drawn at a smaller dot and lower density so it stays a mark, not a picture. This is the most legible card at 300 px by a distance, and the lightest file, but it gives up the specimen, which is the one thing the site has that other sites do not.

## Recommendation

Plate. It is the page itself at card size: warm white, the specimen in its true colours, the rule and the tracked plate line, the ratings as the site's own tags. The title holds at 300 px, the garnet tags still read as two red marks, and the specimen carries the identity where Title has only type. Ledger is the richer object and would make a fine chapter-page hero or PDF title, but on a card the stage index competes with the title for the eye without being readable at feed size. If the author wants Ledger's "04" without its index, the plate direction can take a light Fraunces chapter numeral above the title in place of the eyebrow.

## What `build/cards.py` must change

- Tokens: replace `DARK` with the light sheet from `qa/brand-build.md` (`bg #f7f6f2`, `ink #14181f`, `ink2 #3b424c`, `muted #5c636d`, `line #d8d4cc`, `line2 #bfb9af`, `accent #7a1f2b`). The gradient `.art::before` goes; the art sits on the bare ground.
- Colours: `build/cards-art.js` resolves the specimen's colours for the dark ground (`PC.resolveColors(s, true)`); it must pass `false` for the light ground (see the copy here, one word changed). The ink point colour in `render_art` becomes the light ink; the alpha range drops from 30 to 150 to about 28 to 140 so the cloud reads as the page's grey stipple, with accent points at 1.4 times.
- Fonts: add Jost 400 to the Google Fonts link and Source Serif 4 at opsz 8 to 60, weight 400 and 500. The title's `font-variation-settings` keep `"opsz" 144, "SOFT" 0, "WONK" 0`.
- Layout (Plate): the art box becomes 600 by 500 at `right: 36px; top: 30px`, with a `.plate-line` beneath it (Jost 14.5 px, 0.28 em tracking, uppercase, the 28 px centered rule as a `::before`, the number in garnet). The text column stays at `left: 72px`, width 500. The eyebrow becomes "Chip Supply Chain | Chapter NN · Stage n of 15" with a 1 px tick between (the `.label i` rule in the copy). The garnet rule bar is replaced by the two rating tags (`.tags li`: Instrument Sans 500 at 15 px, 0.08 em, uppercase, 1 px garnet border, garnet text). The plate line text is "Specimen NN · <chapter title>"; the views keep a specimen number (home shows 01) and the view's own label.
- The line: the chapter's `oneLiner` is too long for a card; take its first two sentences (`re.split(r'(?<=\.) ', s)[:2]`) and clamp at five lines of 25 px. The existing fit script needs `flex: none` on the line, or a flex column silently squeezes it and the title never shrinks; the copy here also shrinks the title while the whole text column overflows, not only while the title runs past two lines.
- Ratings: the manifest's `line` already reads "Extreme concentration · Very hard to substitute" for chapters; split it on " · " into the two tags. Views have no tags, and take the wordmark (`font-variant-caps: small-caps`, Fraunces 500 at opsz 36, 0.075 em) in place of the eyebrow.
- Sizes: with the cloud on white and 256 colours the PNGs land at 60 to 85 KB at 0.8 density, so the thinning loop rarely runs; keep it.

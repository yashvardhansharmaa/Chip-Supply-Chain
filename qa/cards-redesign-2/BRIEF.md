# Social card redesign, round 2 (18 Sept 2026)

The site was redesigned on 18 Sept: **Ledger in garnet**. Warm white `#f7f6f2`, blue-black ink `#14181f`, garnet `#7a1f2b` as the only accent (dark theme: `#0f1216` ground, `#ece9e2` ink, garnet lifted to `#d4667b`), Fraunces for titles (light weight, large optical size, SOFT and WONK at 0), Source Serif 4 for text, Instrument Sans for labels, Jost for the specimen caption, hairlines, square corners, a ruled ledger column on the left, the specimen over a centered 28 px rule and a tracked capitals label. See `qa/brand-build/final/` for the live pages and `qa/brand-build.md` for the tokens.

The social cards (1200 by 630, one per chapter and per view, 24 in all, generated by `build/cards.py`, which renders each chapter's point-cloud specimen in its true-to-life colors through `build/cards-art.js`) still carry the previous "Dark" direction: a graphite ground, the specimen glowing at right, a rule and the title. The author wants them redesigned to match the new site. Each designer delivers three directions as PNGs for the home card and the Lithography card (six images), a contact sheet, and a README with one paragraph per direction, a recommendation, and what `cards.py` must change. Work in your own folder under `qa/cards-redesign-2/<key>/`; copy `build/cards.py` and `build/cards-art.js` there and adapt the copies (they take a Chrome path and DevTools port; use your own port); do not edit the originals. The specimen clouds and colors come from `build/cards-art.js` (run with a specimen name to dump the cloud and its declared colors). Every card must read at thumbnail size in a LinkedIn or Substack feed, keep the title legible at 300 px wide, carry chipsupplychain.org, use real content, and contain no em dashes. Files under 150 KB after quantize.

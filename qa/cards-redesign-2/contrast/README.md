# Social cards, round 2: contrast (18 Sept 2026)

Three directions for the home card and the Lithography card, rendered by `cards.py` in this folder (an adapted copy of `build/cards.py`; Chrome on port 9422; `cards-art.js` is the same dump script with its root path moved). All six PNGs are 1200 by 630, quantised to 256 colours, between 102 and 141 KB. `sheet.png` is the contact sheet. The elements the three share: the title in Fraunces 400 at opsz 144, the lede in Source Serif 4, the chapter's two ratings as outlined tags in tracked Instrument Sans capitals (the chokepoint card's own tags), chipsupplychain.org at the foot, and on the right the chapter's point cloud drawn with the site's own grain (fine dots at half a pixel, supersampled twice) over the centered 28 px rule and the Jost plate line, "SPECIMEN 04 · LITHOGRAPHY", with the number in the accent. The home card carries the wordmark in Fraunces small caps with the italic tagline, "How AI chips are made", the home page's dek, and the die floorplan under "SPECIMEN 01 · CHIP DESIGN, EDA AND IP".

## Night

The dark theme as a card: `#0f1216` ground, `#ece9e2` type, the lifted garnet `#d4667b` for the ratings and the specimen number, the specimen in its declared dark-ground colours (the 13.5 nm beam in pale blue-violet, the tin plasma in violet-white; copper on the die). It is the closest to what the site's own dark theme shows a reader who opens the link, and the one that keeps every specimen colour as the table in `qa/specimen-colors.md` declares it. In a white feed it is the card that reads as a plate rather than a page. The risk is the one the old cards had: dark cards are common in tech feeds and the ground alone does not say whose it is; here the small caps wordmark, the Fraunces title and the ruled plate line carry that.

## Split

A warm white text panel (`#f7f6f2`, ink `#14181f`, ratings in the garnet `#7a1f2b`) and, past a `#bfb9af` hairline at x = 690, a dark panel holding the specimen. The type is the site's light theme exactly, so the card and the page a reader lands on match; the specimen keeps its dark-ground colours and glows the way it does in the dark theme. The two grounds also solve the thumbnail: at 300 px the light panel with a black title is the most legible of the three, and the dark panel is a distinct shape even when the cloud has dissolved. The cost is a card with two moods; the hairline and the shared baseline (the plate line at the same height as the tags) hold it together, and it is the only direction where the die floorplan, a dense grey cloud, does not fight the type.

## Garnet

The site's second colour as a poster: `#7a1f2b` ground, off-white type, the specimen in off-white with a single bright tone for both accent channels (the specimen's dark-ground accent: the beam's blue-violet on Lithography, copper on the die). Nothing else is coloured; the ratings become off-white outlined tags and the plate number goes off-white. It is unmistakable in a feed, and the restraint (one tracked label, one rule, a lot of ground) keeps it from reading as a promotion. Two cautions: the die floorplan's copper strip is faint against garnet, and a card that is all garnet says less about the chapter's own material than the other two do. It would serve well as the home and view cards, where the specimen is a diagram, less well across seventeen chapters whose specimens were coloured to be true to life.

## Recommendation

Split. It is the site's light theme, which is what the page is for nearly every reader, with the specimen kept in the colours the specimen engineer chose for the dark ground, and it reads at thumbnail better than the other two because the title sits in black on warm white. Night is the safe second. Garnet is worth keeping for the home card alone if the author wants one poster in the set.

## What `build/cards.py` must change

- Tokens: replace `DARK` with the light set for the text panel (`bg #f7f6f2`, `ink #14181f`, `ink2 #3b424c`, `muted #5c636d`, `line2 #bfb9af`, `accent #7a1f2b`) and a dark panel (`#0f1216`, plate text `#c3c7cd`, number `#d4667b`) drawn as a `.panel` div from x = 690 with a 1 px left border, in place of the `.art` gradient fade. For Night, keep one dark set with `bg #0f1216`, `ink #ece9e2`, `accent #d4667b`.
- Fonts: add Jost 400 to the Google Fonts link and pin Fraunces SOFT and WONK at 0 with the full opsz range (the link in `src/template.html`).
- Title: Fraunces 400 at `"opsz" 144`, line-height 1.0, letter-spacing -0.012em, 92 px shrinking to 56; the garnet bar rule under the title goes.
- Lede: the chapter's `oneLiner` cut to its first two sentences (the second to its first clause when the pair passes 135 characters), Source Serif 4 at 27 px, clamped to three lines. The manifest's `line` (the ratings joined with a middle dot) becomes two outlined tags from the front matter's `card.concentration` and `card.substitutability.band`, Instrument Sans 500 at 15 px, 0.1em, in the accent.
- Specimen: a `.spec` block at x = 726, width 438, art 430 px tall, then a centered 28 px by 1 px rule and the plate line in Jost 13 px, 0.28em tracking, uppercase, with the chapter number in the accent; `LABEL` becomes a plate table of (number, chapter title) and the views take the number of the chapter their specimen belongs to.
- Art: `render_art` at S = 2 with radius `(0.7 + 0.6 * zr) * S`, alpha 20 to 120 and an accent boost of 1.5, density 0.55; the dots then match the site's canvas rather than the old glow. The die floorplan lands at 141 KB, so the thinning loop stays.
- Home: the eyebrow is replaced by the wordmark (`Chip Supply Chain` in Fraunces 500 at opsz 36, `font-variant-caps: small-caps`, 0.075em, with the tagline in Source Serif italic) and the title and dek come from the home page (`How AI chips are made`, `An introduction to the entire semiconductor supply chain.`), which means `build/modules/social.js`'s home entry should carry those two strings instead of the site name, or `cards.py` overrides them as this copy does.

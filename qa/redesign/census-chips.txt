   - B200 Dense, no sparsity, derived from Nvidia’s HGX B200 board table (8 GPUs: 36 PFLOPS FP16/BF16 and 72 PFLOPS FP8 with sparsity, 72 PFLOPS FP4 dense). The higher-power GB200 NVL72 variant reaches 10,000 dense FP4 TFLOPS per GPU. HGX B200 baseboards carry 180 GB per GPU, under the 192 GB quoted for the chip. All three formats are supported and published. The HGX table carries FP4, FP8/FP6, INT8, FP16/BF16, TF32, FP32 and FP64 rows for the board.
   - Ascend 910C Derived from a system figure, with no vendor datasheet and no independent measurement. SemiAnalysis’s CloudMatrix 384 figure of 300 PFLOPS dense BF16 across 384 chips implies about 781 TFLOPS per chip. Epoch AI records 800. Huawei publishes no per-chip figure for the 910C. Its Atlas 900 A3 SuperPoD page gives 307.2, 288.7 or 240.3 PFLOPS FP16 across 384 chips, depending on the model, which is 800, 752 or 626 TFLOPS per chip. Huawei’s own roadmap says eight- and four-bit floats arrive with the 950 series: “Compared with the Ascend 910B and Ascend 910C, our newer chips – starting with the Ascend 950 chips – … will support more data formats, including FP32, HF32, FP16, BF16, FP8, MXFP8, HiF8, MXFP4, and HiF4.” So the FP8 and FP4 cells read not supported.

# Redesign, stage 2: the chapter page

Branch `redesign` in the build checkout. Built to `qa/redesign/BRIEF.md` (main checkout), the decisions the lead added for this stage, the hybrid mockup's chapter (`qa/design3/directions/hybrid/chapter.html`) and the review images `r8-body-1.png`, `r8-body-2.png`, `r8-body-phone.png` and `r5-parts-in-caption.png`. One stylesheet, `src/modules/chapter.css`, draws the chapter in both its homes: The Chain in the app, and the 17 readable pages `dist/<chapter>/` (social.js inlines it after `build/assets/chapter-page.css`).

## What was built

**The chapter as a 12-column grid** (`build/build.js` `chapterHTML`, used for all 17 chapters; chapter 17 no longer has its own copy in `src/template.html`):

1. **The opener.** A Fragment Mono kicker ("Chapter 04", "Stage 4 of 15", then the band as a mark and a word: a solid bar for Extreme, an outline for High; chapters 16 and 17 read "The economics" and "The contest"); the title in Fraunces 350, up to 116 px (a title over 16 characters takes two lines at up to 74 px); the standfirst (`oneLiner`) in Literata beside it; the facts line in mono ("2,391 words / 10 min · Specimen: EUV scanner · Updated … · 49 sources"); the cite line as the site's `.link` (Link to chapter, Cite this chapter, Chapter PDF); an ink rule under it all.
2. **The specimen.** A slot under the opener (columns 1 to 8) with the caption flush left beside the drawing's foot (columns 9 to 12): "Specimen 04 · Lithography" in mono and the chapter's `specimenCaption`, verbatim, in Literata. The site's one WebGL canvas moves into the slot of whichever chapter's slot is most in view (`Stage.host`, `Sections.hostSpec` in `src/app.js`); the stage beside the page stands down on The Chain, and the canvas goes back to it on any other view. Drag and the arrow keys turn it as before, and its pause button moves with it.
3. **The chokepoint card**, full width on the paper tint: "Chokepoint card" and "Lithography, stage 4 of 15" over an ink rule; the two ratings in Fraunces at up to 76 px with the band's mark before Extreme or High, the price at 26 px; Who leads beside Where it is made as code, name and value (the codes plain mono, no boxes); Why substitution is slow across; China beside the US.
4. **The body.** The contents rail (columns 1 to 3, sticky): On this page (In plain terms, the chapter's sections, Flashcards, Sources; the section in view marked by a wine rule), Figures (number and short title), and The chain (the 15 stages with their Extreme and High marks, the current one in wine, then chapters 16 and 17). Beside it: In plain terms as a Fraunces section heading over Literata at 21 px, In short, then the text. Each section opens on an ink rule with its number in mono above a Fraunces heading (a CSS counter, so the text is untouched). Under 1200 px the rail becomes a line of links over the text.

**Figures** (`figure()` in `build/build.js`; 59 in all): a white frame with "Figure 04.5" in mono, the finding as the large line and the title under it (all 44 data figures now carry a `finding` from main's clarity pass); a figure without one shows its title's first sentence as the large line and any further sentences under it. Bars follow rule B: the largest value solid ink with a bold label, the rest the light tint, a remainder ("Other", "All others") hatched; stacked shares are drawn as bars, one per share, as the hybrid draws them, so no legend is needed and no country is ever colored. Under each chart, a source strip on the paper tint: Source, the label and its footnote number in brackets (it opens the same source card as the marks in the text), any Note, the figure's own address (`chipsupplychain.org/lithography/#fig-lithography-asml-region`), and Cite (the figure's citation opens in place, as the chapter's does), Table (the figure's own rows in place of the bars), CSV (a download of the rows, no script needed) and PNG (the figure redrawn at 1,600 px on the light ground). An animation gets "Figure 04.1 · Explainer, 19 seconds" and Cite; a timeline Cite and CSV. `FIG_JS` in build.js serves both the app and the readable pages.

**Lithography's annotated drawing, on the live specimen.** The renderer projects the four parts every frame (`Stage.balloons`, `Stage.drawBalloons`): each balloon stands in a column clear of the drawing on the side its part is on, at the part's height, with a leader to each point (two for the mirrors); a part that crosses to the other side as the drawing turns moves its balloon across (with a margin, so it never flickers), and a name that would run past the drawing's edge goes under its balloon. The data is in `data/specimen-parts.json`, keyed by chapter id: number, name, maker or the stage that covers it, and the part's points in the specimen's own units (`src/kit.js` now keeps the transform it normalizes with, so those points land on the drawing). The parts list under the caption reads, verbatim from the brief: Light source: ASML San Diego (US), laser by Trumpf (DE); Mirrors: Zeiss SMT (DE); Mask: see stage 05, Photomasks and Pellicles; Wafer: see stage 02, Silicon and Wafers (both links). The home shows no balloons. The readable page, which has no live drawing, shows the hybrid's annotated still (`build/assets/specimens/lithography-annotated.webp`, 87 KB) with the same balloons drawn from the file's `still` entry, a numbers-only version on a phone, and the still turned light-on-dark in the dark theme.

**Chapters 16 and 17** draw their specimen on the night ground (`--night` tokens): a dark plate behind the drawing and caption, the drawing in the night ink and the specimen's dark colors (`Stage.refreshColors` reads the colors where the canvas sits).

**Flashcards** keep their deck and are restyled: "Flashcards" as a section heading, paper cards, the question in Literata 26 px, the answer in Literata 600, pill buttons. Sources fold under an ink rule with "Sources (49)" as a section heading. Previous and Next close each chapter as two quiet lines (the next chapter follows at once on The Chain; on a readable page Next is set large).

**The phone** (under 900 px): one column in reading order (opener, card, On this page, In plain terms, In short, text); the specimen stays in the strip at the foot of the screen, with the parts list in its caption once expanded. The masthead goes up as the reader scrolls down a chapter and comes back on the way up (a jump never hides it), and once the opener has gone a slim bar at the top names the chapter and the section ("04 Lithography · Who makes it"), a link back to the chapter's top. Links land clear of the slim bar.

**The readable pages** (`build/modules/social.js`): the same opener (with "Open the interactive chapter" under the cite line), specimen caption (and "See it in 3D in the interactive chapter"), card, rail, In plain terms, In short, figures with their strips, review questions, Previous and Next, and sources. The 15-bar strip under the head is gone; the rail carries the chain. Figure Cite links open their citation without scripts (`:target`).

**Decisions applied from the lead's notes**
- The stage component's caption sits right under the drawing (`Stage.fit` measures how far the drawing reaches over a full turn and sets `--slack`): 10 px under the drawing's lowest point on the home at 1440, against about 150 before. The home's "High" legend mark stands on its line.
- The stage 1 review's fixes: labels that leaned on CSS capitals are written in sentence case at their source (the Export Controls countdown, now "In 15 days" in ink; AI Chips' key "Logic die, Memory stack, Interposer, Substrate" and "AI chips · Package"; the walk's and the supplier table's "Source"; Suppliers' "Estimate"); Introduction's line reads "A short read through the whole chain, about 11 min"; Who leads codes are plain text on the home and the card; on the home the stage now follows the evidence figure in the page's order, so Tab reaches the 15 marks before the specimen.
- Main merged twice (42b5288, the clarity pass and the findings; a3e52b0, the flashcards' plain questions). Only build outputs conflicted (dist/, build/page-dates.json); both were rebuilt. No conflict in content/ or src/. The two test findings were never committed.

## Census differences

Only the home (`#/`), The Chain (`#/chain`) and the 17 readable chapter pages differ beyond letter case, and every difference is one the decisions require:

1. **The home.** The stage 1 differences stand (the evidence figure, the field and the two ways in, Lithography's specimen first). New in stage 2: "17 paragraphs, 2,508 words, about 11 min" under Introduction is now "A short read through the whole chain, about 11 min", as the lead asked.
2. **The opener** (The Chain and the readable pages). The ledger tile's "Chapter" / "04" / "stage 4 of 15" becomes the kicker "Chapter 04", "Stage 4 of 15" and the band's word ("Extreme", "High"; "The economics" and "The contest" for chapters 16 and 17). The facts line gains "49 sources"; the readable pages' facts line gains "Specimen: EUV scanner", as The Chain's has. The dots between the cite line's actions ("·") go; the actions are spaced instead.
3. **The specimen's caption.** The Chain's caption, which lived in the stage beside the page, is now page text under each chapter's opener ("Specimen 04 · Lithography" and the chapter's `specimenCaption`, verbatim). Lithography adds "Parts, as numbered on the drawing" and the four parts. The readable pages' "Specimen · EUV scanner" label becomes "Specimen 04 · Lithography".
4. **The card.** Its head adds "Lithography, stage 4 of 15". Who leads and Where it is made now set code, name and value in their own columns, so "USUnited States …" and "NLASML …" (one run-together line before) read as three; no word goes.
5. **The rail** adds "On this page", the chapter's section names, "Flashcards" (or "Review questions" on a readable page), "Sources", "Figures" with each figure's number and title, and "The chain", "Extreme", "High" and the 17 chapter names, once per chapter. Under 1200 px only On this page shows.
6. **The readable pages lose the 15-bar strip** under the head, which the rail replaces: its numbers 01 to 15, "Lithography, stage 4 of 15", "Next stage: 05 Photomasks and Pellicles" (or "Last stage in the chain"), and its key "Concentration · Extreme · High". The rail and Previous and Next carry the same links.
7. **Figures** (59). Each adds its number ("Figure 04.5"; an animation "Figure 04.1 · Explainer, 19 seconds"), its address and Cite, CSV, and for bars Table and PNG. "Source: X" becomes "Source X [26]"; an animation's note gains "Note" before it. The 44 findings from main's clarity pass are the large lines, with each title under them; a title of more than one sentence without a finding is split after its first sentence, which drops that sentence's full stop and adds one at the end of the line under it (no word changes). Stacked shares are now bars, so "EUV48%" (the legend's run-together name and value) reads "EUV" and "48%".
8. **Previous and Next** keep their words; the readable page's "Next chapter" stays.

Words the census lists as lost on these pages are all of four kinds: run-together tokens that are now separate lines ("usunited", "nlasml", "euv48%", "minupdated"); the strip's words above ("next", "concentration", "stage", "last", the stage numbers); "chapter" from the tile; and on the home the lead's new Introduction line. No sentence of chapter text, no figure value and no source is missing.

## Known issues and open questions

- **Footnote order on the card.** The card now comes before In plain terms, as in the mockup, but its footnote numbers still follow the old reading order (In plain terms, In short, then the card), so the card's marks run 5, 4, 3, 9, 10 … as the mockup's did. Renumbering in the new order would change most of each chapter's numbers and its source list's order, so I left it for the lead to decide.
- **Space above the drawing.** The camera frames each specimen's bounding sphere, so a flat or narrow specimen leaves 80 to 130 px of empty canvas between the opener's rule and the drawing at 1440. The caption is pulled to the drawing's foot (`--slack`); the top band stays, since trimming it would mean a crop projection and a slot height per specimen.
- **Figure actions by type.** Table and PNG are on the bar charts (42 of 59 figures). A timeline has Cite and CSV, an animation Cite only.
- **Print.** The print path's hooks are kept (`.chapter-head .plate`, the article's own `.prose`, `.ch-ledger .chain-track`, a print-only chapter tile), and the rail, the drawing's slot and the figure strips' actions are hidden for print, but the PDFs were not made again: stage 4 restyles `print.css` and should check the stage index, which now reads full chapter names.
- **The Chain's top** (How to read a chapter, Contents) keeps the old layout at a 1,000 px reading width until stage 3; the stage beside the page no longer shows there.
- **The readable pages' rail** does not mark the section in view (it has no script for it); the app's does.
- **Size estimates.** `contain-intrinsic-height` for The Chain's chapters is measured again for the new page (12,500 px at 1440, 16,900 px on a phone), so the scroll bar and deep links settle as before.

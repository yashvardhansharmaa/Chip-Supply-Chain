# Brand strategy: options, not changes

Read-only review of the 10 September build; screenshot `qa/brand/brand-strategy-masthead.png`. Domains checked by `whois` today.

## Verdict

**Works.** The site earns "chokepoint" as method, not slogan: all seventeen chapters carry a rated `substitutability` and a concentration band, and the policy critic found it "reads as analysis, not advocacy" (`qa/crit-staffer.md`).

**Weakest**, in order:

1. **Name and hero point opposite ways.** The wordmark promises leverage, the h1 manufacturing ("How AI chips are made"). Nothing in the top 200 pixels joins them.
2. **The tagline repeats the subtitle.** "The AI chip supply chain" sits one line above "Every stage of the supply chain, and who controls it." Two of four brand lines, the same six words.
3. **"Chokepoint" is unglossed at the door.** The generalist tester: "*What is a 'chokepoint'?* Used four times here, defined nowhere here" (`qa/nav-test-generalist.md`).
4. Casing drift: the home door reads "Export controls", the tab "Export Controls".

## 1. Name and wordmark

| Name | Staffer | Researcher | Journalist | Domain | Advocacy risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Compute Chokepoints** (baseline) | The field's own word | Citable, signals a thesis | Needs explaining | computechokepoints.com free | Medium. The controls literature's frame: a place to squeeze. In Washington it also collides with Operation Choke Point. |
| **How AI Chips Are Made** | An explainer, not a reference | Not a name you cite | Best; matches what people search | howaichipsaremade.com and howgpusaremade.com free | None, and no standing. Undersells the board and the data pages. |
| **Chokepoint Atlas** | Keeps the hook | "Atlas" says reference, not argument; the site is atlas-shaped | Vivid, quotable | chokepointatlas.com free | Low. The noun defuses the adjective. |
| **The Compute Chain** | Neutral, loses the hook | Clean, no frame to defend | Bland | thecomputechain.com free; computechain.com taken | None. Gives up the memorable word. |

## 2. Hero pairs

Current: "How AI chips are made" / "Every stage of the supply chain, and who controls it."

| # | Title / subtitle | Rationale | Serves |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | How AI chips are made / Fifteen stages, 220 firms, every number linked to its source. | Trades the repeated "supply chain" for the differentiator: 702 titled sources. | Researcher, journalist |
| B | How AI chips are made / Every stage, who holds it, and what could replace it. | Names the three fields on the chokepoint card. | Staffer |
| C | The chain behind one chip / From quartz to a rack, and who holds each stage. | Matches the home page's route of one chip. | Generalist |
| D | Who controls the AI chain / Fifteen stages, the firms that hold each, and the rules. | Puts the name's promise in the hero. | Staffer, researcher |
| E | Every chokepoint in AI compute / Fifteen stages, 220 firms, and the rules that gate them. | Wordmark and hero finally agree. | Staffer |

**Overpromising.** E: "every" is false, since five matrix rows carry null shares by policy. D: "controls" invites the decision question the staffer says the site cannot answer: where the rules stand yes, what to decide next no. A, B and C are delivered in full. Start here is also titled "How AI chips are made", so a title change moves two places.

## 3. Masthead tagline

Current: "The AI chip supply chain", a category that duplicates the subtitle.

1. **"Who makes each part, and who controls it"**: function; bridges wordmark to hero.
2. **"Fifteen stages, 220 firms, 35 chips"**: scale; reads as a reference work.
3. **"Stage by stage, from quartz to a rack"**: method, in the site's own words.

## 5. Positioning

CSET's supply-chain work is the authority but arrives as PDFs and stops at the segment. SemiAnalysis is faster, paid and written for the trade; here it is a source, not a peer. *Chip War* is a history that ends where this begins. Humanity's Last Machine supplied the form without the citations. This site is the free, present-tense reference: fifteen stages in process order, a rated card on each, 220 firms, 35 chips, a ten-flow board of what can legally cross, China revenue from filings. It reports; it neither scoops nor argues.

**Sentence to use:** "A free, sourced walk through the fifteen stages of the AI chip supply chain: who holds each one, how hard it would be to replace, and what the export controls now allow."

## Recommended set

- **Name: Compute Chokepoints, unchanged.** The only option that says who the site is for; the domain is free.
- **Tagline: option 1.** The fix that matters: it joins *makes* in the hero to *controls* in the wordmark and clears the repeat.
- **Hero: pair A.** With the tagline carrying control, the subtitle carries scale and sourcing, which is what a researcher decides on.
- **One tab change: "AI Chips" to "Compare chips",** plus the door casing.

Four lines, four jobs: who it is for, what it covers, the plain question, the evidence.

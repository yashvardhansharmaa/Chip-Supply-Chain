# Social card redesign: five directions

Mockups only; no site file changed. Ten PNGs at 1200 by 630, one per direction for the home card and the Lithography chapter, plus `sheet.png` (all ten at half size) and the build in `mock/` (`build.py` renders the art with PIL, writes the HTML, shoots it with headless Chrome the way `build/cards.py` does, and lays out the sheet; `dump-specimen.js` pulls a specimen's point cloud out of `src/kit.js` for the dark card). Every title, band, town and number comes from `content/*.md` front matter or `data/suppliers.json`. All ten carry chipsupplychain.org, use the site's tokens and three faces, and were checked at 300 px wide on the sheet.

## 1. Floorplan (`floorplan-*.png`)

The die floorplan point cloud from `qa/die-floorplan.bin`, at the cover's angle, bleeds off the top, right and bottom behind a gradient, with the eyebrow, title, amber rule and band line on the left. On the home card the die-to-die interface keeps its amber; on a chapter card the die is drawn in ink and the fifteen slices along its long axis stand for the fifteen stages, with the chapter's slice lit, so stage 4 of 15 is a stripe a quarter of the way up the die. The foot names the lead firm and its town. It scales to 24 cards with one shared cloud and a slice index per stage; the two view cards and the non-stage chapters (16, 17) take the home treatment. Cost: about 60 lines of PIL in `cards.py` (or a pre-rendered PNG per slice, fifteen files), a few seconds per card, and the PNG lands at 310 to 330 KB after the 256-colour quantise, over the 150 KB target, so the cloud would need to be thinned or the dots enlarged before shipping.

## 2. Route (`route-*.png`)

The card the existing template already almost is, with a fifteen-stop route strip across the foot: a rail, fifteen stops numbered 01 to 15, and on a chapter card the stops already passed filled, the chapter's stop lit in amber with its number, title and town above it (04 Lithography, Veldhoven, NL). The home card shows the whole strip unlit, with the first and last stage titles at its ends. It scales cleanly: the strip is pure HTML and CSS, the only per-chapter inputs are the stage index and a town, and the town needs one short field per chapter (the mock takes the last clause of the first country note, which only Lithography states as a town, so 14 chapters would want a `card.town` string). Non-stage pages get the unlit strip. Cost: an afternoon in `cards.py`, no new dependency, PNGs under 60 KB.

## 3. Numeral (`numeral-*.png`)

The chapter number set at 400 px is the composition, "/ 15" beneath it, the title on the right with the concentration band as a filled tag (amber for Extreme, graphite for High) and the substitutability band as an outlined tag, and the chapter's first sentence under them. The home card sets 15 with "stages" and tags for 17 chapters and 220 firms. It reads at thumbnail size before anything else here does, and it says what the site is about (a ranked, numbered chain) without art. It scales without extra inputs: every field is already in the front matter; view cards need a number or a word to fill the slot (chapters, firms, moves for Export Controls). Cost: a template change only, PNGs about 50 KB, half a day. The weakness is sameness across 17 chapters: a feed of these looks like a page-number series.

## 4. Dark (`dark-*.png`)

The site's dark tokens (graphite ground, pale ink, the brighter amber), the amber rule, and one specimen silhouette on the right: the chapter's own specimen, dumped from `src/kit.js` through `mock/dump-specimen.js` and drawn in pale ink with the accent points in amber. Lithography gets the EUV scanner in section with its light path lit; home gets the GPU die. Each chapter already names a specimen in its front matter, so this scales to all 17 with no new data, and each card looks different, which the other four do not manage. View cards need a specimen assigned (the die for home, the globe for Geopolitics-style views, the rack for Systems). Cost: a Node step to dump each cloud and the PIL renderer from `qa/cover/render.py` folded into `cards.py` (or 24 pre-rendered art PNGs committed under `build/assets/`), a fixed tilt per specimen chosen by eye, and PNGs at 160 to 240 KB after quantising, so the density needs trimming to meet 150 KB. It also breaks the light-on-wafer-grey identity of the site, which is either the point or the objection.

## 5. Data (`data-*.png`)

A number card: the chapter's headline figure at 340 px with a Newsreader sentence beside it that says what the number is and why it matters (48 EUV scanners booked as revenue in 2025, every one built by a single firm; ASML annual report 2025 credited at the foot), the title small above it. Home sets 15 stages. It is the most shareable of the five for policy readers, because the card makes a claim, and it is the most expensive to scale: 17 chapters need 17 chosen numbers and 17 sentences that survive a fact check, none of which exist yet as a field (`keyFacts` has candidates but no pick), and view cards need their own (220 firms, the export-control move count). Cost: a template change plus a `card.headline` object per chapter, an editorial day, PNGs under 60 KB.

## Recommendation

Ship direction 2 (Route) now and keep direction 4 (Dark) as the follow-up. Route costs an afternoon, needs one new short field per chapter, keeps the site's identity and the existing type, stays under 60 KB, and answers the author's complaint: the strip shows where the chapter sits in the chain, names its town, and gives the feed something amber to catch. Dark is the one people would stop scrolling for, and the specimens are already drawn, but it needs the cloud pipeline in the build, per-specimen tilts, and a file-size pass, and it should be a deliberate decision to put the site's dark mode on every unfurl. If only one change is possible, Route; if two, Route with the Data card's headline number added to the chapter cards later, once the 17 numbers are chosen.

How to read a chapter

Every chapter opens with a chokepoint card. Three fields on it matter most.

  • Concentration. How many firms credibly supply this stage: Extreme, High, Moderate or Low.
  • Chokepoint score. 1 means many substitutes. 5 means a single point of failure, where losing one supplier stops leading-edge production worldwide.
  • Substitution time. Our estimate of how long a well-funded state or firm would need to stand up an alternative. It is the most opinionated number on the card.

The card also gives China's position and the United States'. The chapters run in process order, and every number links to its source. The scoring method is on the Methods page.

AI Chips

The chips that train and run AI models in 2026, with the foundry, process node, memory supplier and packaging plant behind each one. Pick two to four chips: the page sets their specs side by side and draws their packages at one scale. The specimen beside the text follows whichever chip you click.

Packages at one scale

Every package is drawn from above at one scale, so a bigger one looks bigger. The logic dies do the computing, the memory stacks feed them, the interposer is the silicon slab that wires the two together, and the substrate is the card underneath. Click a package to highlight that chip in the table and open its notes.

Specs side by side

Hover any figure to see its source. TFLOPS counts trillions of calculations a second. FP16, FP8 and FP4 are number formats: the fewer the bits, the more calculations a chip gets through.

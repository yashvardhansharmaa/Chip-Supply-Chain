    The Chain

    This page covers each of the fifteen stages of making an AI chip in detail, one chapter per stage, in the order a chip is made, from design software to the data center. Two more chapters at the end look across all the stages, at what a chip costs and who controls the chain. To help readers learn, each chapter has a 3D model, most have an animation, and every one ends with flashcards.

    How to read a chapter

    Each stage chapter opens with a chokepoint card. It rates the stage on two scales, gives a price or market size, and says where China and the United States stand.

    • Concentration. How many firms could supply the stage today. Every stage rates Extreme (three or fewer firms, or one with more than half) or High (four to six).
    • Substitutability. How hard it would be for a well-funded state or firm to build an alternative, rated Very hard, Hard or Moderately hard.
    • Price or market size. What one unit costs, or what the stage earns in a year.

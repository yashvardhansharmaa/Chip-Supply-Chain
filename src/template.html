What can be soldNo licenseProhibitedThe state on the date above. The full key is under the board.
  • No licenseNone needed.
  • RestrictedA government limits sales to some buyers or uses without banning them outright.
  • Case by caseA license is required, and each application is judged on its own.
  • Presumption of denialA license is required and will normally be refused. This is the usual policy for those on the Entity List, a US list of restricted foreign companies, institutes and individuals.
  • ProhibitedNo license is available, or the item is banned outright.
  • PolicyMoney and tariffs, which do not stop a shipment.
  • SuspendedThe rule is on hold until the date shown. When the hold ends, the state beside it takes effect.

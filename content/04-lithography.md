# Lithography

ASML sold 327 lithography systems in 2025, 48 of them extreme ultraviolet (EUV) ([source](https://ourbrand.asml.com/m/419103cb23dfeaa4/original/asml-2025-annual-report-financial-performance-section.pdf)). Nobody else makes EUV systems ([source](https://ourbrand.asml.com/m/8ab959d4926657b/original/asml-2025-annual-report-strategic-report-section.pdf)).

## How it works

Lithography prints the pattern of a circuit onto the wafer. A machine holds a stencil of one layer of the circuit up to a lamp and projects the image, four times smaller, onto the silicon. The shorter the wavelength of the light, the finer the lines a machine can print. A machine can still print lines several times narrower than its light's wavelength, and ASML says its 193-nanometer argon fluoride laser light prints features as small as 38 nanometers ([source](https://www.asml.com/en/technology/lithography-principles/light-and-lasers)). Air soaks up extreme ultraviolet light, the shortest in use, so machines that print with it work in a vacuum. Only ASML, a Dutch company, makes them. Export controls are government rules on who a company may sell to. Every advanced chip passes through ASML's machines, so export controls on ASML affect all advanced chipmaking.

The stencil is called a photomask, and the machine a scanner (see [Photomasks and pellicles](#photomasks-and-pellicles)).

The wafer is coated with photoresist, a film that changes wherever light touches it. The mask holds one layer of the circuit as a pattern of clear and dark areas, drawn at four times its size on the chip. The machine shines light through the mask, shrinks the image four times with a lens, and lands it on the film.

A wash then removes the film where the light hit, leaving the pattern standing on the wafer. The next machine etches that pattern into the wafer or fills it with metal. Then the film is stripped and the next mask goes in. A chip takes dozens of masks, one per layer.

{{figure:litho-print}}

The image from one mask covers a patch about 26 by 33 mm. The wafer moves under the lens one patch at a time, close to a hundred patches per wafer. ASML's own wafers-per-hour rating for an EUV scanner assumes 96 of them ([source](https://www.asml.com/en/products/euv-lithography-systems/twinscan-nxe3400b)). Before the wafer is exposed, the machine measures alignment marks printed in earlier layers so the new layer lines up with them ([source](https://www.asml.com/en/products/euv-lithography-systems/twinscan-nxe3400b)). ASML rates the NXE:3400B's matched-machine overlay, a measure of that alignment, at 2 nm ([source](https://www.asml.com/en/products/euv-lithography-systems/twinscan-nxe3400b)).

Finer lines need shorter light. Glass and air both absorb the shortest light in use, at 13.5 nm ([source](https://www.asml.com/en/products/euv-lithography-systems)). So an EUV scanner works in a vacuum and uses mirrors instead of lenses, and its masks are mirrors too ([source](https://www.asml.com/en/products/euv-lithography-systems)). Older machines use light with a wavelength more than fourteen times longer ([source](https://www.asml.com/en/technology/lithography-principles/light-and-lasers)). Immersion scanners, one type of older machine, fill the gap between lens and wafer with water to sharpen the image ([source](https://www.asml.com/en/technology/lithography-principles/lenses-and-mirrors)).

When one exposure cannot draw a pattern finely enough, the layer is printed with two, three or four masks instead. Each extra pass costs a mask, machine time and a chance to misalign. ASML says one EUV exposure per layer cuts masks and process steps and lowers cost ([source](https://ourbrand.asml.com/m/8ab959d4926657b/original/asml-2025-annual-report-strategic-report-section.pdf)).

## Variants and trade-offs

### Deep ultraviolet

Deep ultraviolet (DUV) light comes from gas lasers: 248 nanometers from krypton fluoride (KrF), 193 from argon fluoride (ArF), shone through air or water ([source](https://www.asml.com/en/technology/lithography-principles/light-and-lasers)). Most of the machines sold are still DUV tools. Of the systems ASML sold in 2025, 131 were ArF immersion, 78 KrF and 16 ArF dry, all deep ultraviolet, and another 54 were older i-line tools ([source](https://ourbrand.asml.com/m/3136300aa4999bc1/original/2026_01_28_Presentation-Investor-Relations-Q4-2025.pdf)). The i-line tools use 365 nm light from a mercury lamp, and ASML says they print features as small as 220 nm ([source](https://www.asml.com/en/technology/lithography-principles/light-and-lasers)).

Reuters puts a DUV scanner at roughly $60 million ([source](https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/250-million-asml-printer-behind-nvidias-chips-2026-07-28/)). In DUV, ASML competes with Nikon and Canon, and it still holds above 80 percent of that market ([source](https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/250-million-asml-printer-behind-nvidias-chips-2026-07-28/)). Among DUV tools, export controls focus on immersion scanners, because without EUV a fab needs NXT:2000i-class scanners and several passes per layer to make leading-edge logic chips. Dutch export licensing covers these scanners.

### Extreme ultraviolet

The 13.5 nm light comes from molten tin droplets shot from a generator ([source](https://www.asml.com/en/technology/lithography-principles/light-and-lasers)). A laser hits each droplet twice, first with a low-intensity pulse that flattens it and then with a more powerful one that vaporizes it into plasma ([source](https://www.asml.com/en/technology/lithography-principles/light-and-lasers)). The process repeats 50,000 times a second ([source](https://www.asml.com/en/technology/lithography-principles/light-and-lasers)). Mirrors made by Zeiss, a German optics company, carry the light to the wafer ([source](https://www.asml.com/en/technology/lithography-principles/lenses-and-mirrors)). The largest are a meter across and smooth to within tens of picometers, a picometer being a thousandth of a nanometer ([source](https://www.asml.com/en/technology/lithography-principles/lenses-and-mirrors)).

Brighter light means more wafers an hour, and ASML ran the first 1,000-watt EUV source in April 2025 ([source](https://ourbrand.asml.com/m/8ab959d4926657b/original/asml-2025-annual-report-strategic-report-section.pdf)). Its TWINSCAN NXE:3800E ships at its full 220 wafers an hour, 37 percent more than the NXE:3600D ([source](https://ourbrand.asml.com/m/8ab959d4926657b/original/asml-2025-annual-report-strategic-report-section.pdf)).

### High-NA EUV

High-NA scanners raise the numerical aperture (NA), a measure of how well the optics collect and focus light, from 0.33 to 0.55 ([source](https://www.asml.com/en/company/stories/2024/5-things-high-na-euv)). That cuts the smallest printable feature from 13 nm to 8 nm ([source](https://www.asml.com/en/company/stories/2024/5-things-high-na-euv)). Their optics shrink the mask image four times in one direction and eight in the other, halving the patch of wafer one exposure covers and doubling the exposures per wafer ([source](https://www.asml.com/en/company/stories/2024/5-things-high-na-euv)). The second-generation TWINSCAN EXE:5200B runs at 175 wafers an hour, 60 percent above the EXE:5000 ([source](https://ourbrand.asml.com/m/8ab959d4926657b/original/asml-2025-annual-report-strategic-report-section.pdf)).

Reuters puts a standard EUV tool at around $200 million and a High-NA machine at $350 to $400 million, prices ASML does not publish ([source](https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/250-million-asml-printer-behind-nvidias-chips-2026-07-28/)).

{{figure:tool-price}}

Intel and TSMC have chosen differently on High-NA:

- **Intel Foundry** says it uses High-NA in volume production for select layers of some Intel 18A products ([source](https://www.intel.com/content/www/us/en/newsroom/news/intel-foundry/intel-foundry-asml-accelerate-industry-readiness-for-high-na-euv.html)). It installed the first EXE:5200B ([source](https://www.asml.com/en/news/press-releases/2026/high-na-euv-reaches-new-readiness-milestone)). By September 2026 it had run more than one million wafers on High-NA tools, a count that includes tool testing and research as well as volume production ([source](https://www.intel.com/content/www/us/en/newsroom/news/intel-foundry/intel-foundry-asml-accelerate-industry-readiness-for-high-na-euv.html)).
- **TSMC** waited, and in September 2026 said it will use High-NA in high-volume manufacturing from 2030 ([source](https://pr.tsmc.com/english/news/3338)).

ASML sold four High-NA (EXE) systems in 2025, against two in 2024 ([source](https://ourbrand.asml.com/m/419103cb23dfeaa4/original/asml-2025-annual-report-financial-performance-section.pdf)). In its 2025 annual report, filed in February 2026, ASML said it expected High-NA to start supporting high-volume manufacturing in 2027 ([source](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/937966/000162828026011377/asml-2025xannualxreportx.htm)). On 8 September 2026 ASML and TSMC announced a move to 12-inch photomasks to remove stitching constraints ([source](https://pr.tsmc.com/english/news/3338)). Stitching constraints are the design limits that come from printing a large chip in two halves. They aim for a pilot mask line by 2031 and 12-inch High-NA systems in production by 2033 ([source](https://pr.tsmc.com/english/news/3338)).

## Who makes it

ASML's net sales were EUR 32.7 billion in 2025, at a 52.8 percent gross margin ([source](https://ourbrand.asml.com/m/419103cb23dfeaa4/original/asml-2025-annual-report-financial-performance-section.pdf)). In July 2026 it raised its 2026 forecast to EUR 43 to 45 billion, at a gross margin of 54 to 56 percent ([source](https://ourbrand.asml.com/asset/c8dbf3fc-4c5e-4406-83f6-27694b138245/Press-Release-Financial-Results-Q2-2026.pdf)). Its 2025 annual report lists 5,100 suppliers ([source](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/937966/000162828026011377/asml-2025xannualxreportx.htm)). Four of them make the main parts of the EUV machine:

- **Zeiss SMT**, Oberkochen, makes the illumination system and the six-mirror projection optics ([source](https://www.zeiss.com/semiconductor-manufacturing-technology/inspiring-technology/euv-lithography.html)).
- **Trumpf**, Ditzingen, makes the carbon dioxide drive laser, which amplifies a few watts to 40 kilowatts ([source](https://www.trumpf.com/en_US/products/lasers/euv-drive-laser/)).
- **ASML San Diego**, which includes the light-source maker Cymer that ASML bought in 2013, designs the source where the laser turns tin into plasma, and builds the droplet generator ([source](https://www.asml.com/en/company/about-asml/locations/san-diego)).
- **VDL ETG** builds the frames that suspend and position the mirrors ([source](https://web.archive.org/web/20240726075938/https://www.vdlgroep.com/en/news/vdl-etg-builds-complex-frames-for-zeiss)).

ASML says the production capacity of Zeiss, its sole supplier of optics, limits how many lithography systems it can build ([source](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/937966/000162828026011377/asml-2025xannualxreportx.htm)).

Nikon and Canon serve narrower markets. Nikon sold 22 new chip lithography systems in the year to March 2026, in a market it sizes at 570 units for 2025 ([source](https://www.nikon.com/content/dam/web-assets/nikoncom/company/local/global/en/ir/ir_library/result/pdf/2026/26_all_e.pdf)). Its Precision Equipment business made an operating loss in the year to March 2026 ([source](https://www.nikon.com/content/dam/web-assets/nikoncom/company/local/global/en/ir/ir_library/result/pdf/2026/26_all_e.pdf)). Canon's alternative is nanoimprint, where the FPA-1200NZ2C stamps the pattern instead of projecting it, down to a 14 nm linewidth ([source](https://global.canon/en/news/2024/20240926.html)). Canon delivered one in 2024 to the Texas Institute for Electronics, to be used for research and prototypes ([source](https://global.canon/en/news/2024/20240926.html)).

## Controls and China's answer

Export controls bar EUV from Chinese foundries ([source](https://www.csis.org/blogs/strategic-technologies-blog/breakthroughs-or-boasts-assessing-recent-chinese-lithography)). In September 2025 CSIS judged that EUV remained "firmly out of reach" for China despite massive state investment ([source](https://www.csis.org/blogs/strategic-technologies-blog/breakthroughs-or-boasts-assessing-recent-chinese-lithography)). For DUV scanners and other tools, the restrictions came in this order:

- **Dutch national licensing** of advanced DUV took effect on 1 September 2023 ([source](https://www.government.nl/latest/news/2024/09/06/the-netherlands-expands-export-control-measure-advanced-semiconductor-manufacturing-equipment)).
- **A US rule** published on 25 October 2023 put certain immersion scanners bound for advanced chipmaking under US export rules if they held any American content at all ([source](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2023/10/25/2023-23049/export-controls-on-semiconductor-manufacturing-items)). The rule did not apply if the country the scanners were first exported from already controlled them ([source](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2023/10/25/2023-23049/export-controls-on-semiconductor-manufacturing-items)).
- **A partial license revocation** by the Dutch government, disclosed by ASML on 1 January 2024, stopped shipments of NXT:2050i and NXT:2100i immersion scanners to a few Chinese customers ([source](https://www.asml.com/en/news/press-releases/2023/statement-regarding-partial-revocation-export-license)).
- **The Netherlands widened its licensing** from 7 September 2024 ([source](https://www.government.nl/latest/news/2024/09/06/the-netherlands-expands-export-control-measure-advanced-semiconductor-manufacturing-equipment)). On 15 January 2025 it announced that from 1 April 2025 the license requirement would also cover a very limited number of further goods, among them specific measuring and inspection equipment ([source](https://www.government.nl/latest/news/2025/01/15/klever-export-controls-on-advanced-semiconductor-manufacturing-equipment-to-be-tightened)).
- **The BIS Affiliates Rule**, issued by the US Bureau of Industry and Security, extended entity-list controls to affiliates owned 50 percent or more by listed entities from 29 September 2025 ([source](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2025/09/30/2025-19001/expansion-of-end-user-controls-to-cover-affiliates-of-certain-listed-entities)). BIS then suspended it until 9 November 2026 ([source](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2025/11/12/2025-19846/one-year-suspension-of-expansion-of-end-user-controls-for-affiliates-of-certain-listed-entities)).
- **The EU** added certain Dutch controls to the EU Control List in November 2025 ([source](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/937966/000162828026011377/asml-2025xannualxreportx.htm)). ASML says this changed nothing for its immersion tools, because they already needed a Dutch license ([source](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/937966/000162828026011377/asml-2025xannualxreportx.htm)).

China's share of ASML's net system sales fell from 41 percent in 2024 to 33 percent in 2025 ([source](https://ourbrand.asml.com/m/3136300aa4999bc1/original/2026_01_28_Presentation-Investor-Relations-Q4-2025.pdf)).

On DUV, Reuters reported in July 2026, citing one unnamed source, that Shanghai Aishengna Electronic Technology Group has started producing home-grown immersion DUV tools ([source](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/china-starts-production-home-grown-immersion-duv-chipmaking-tools-source-2026-07-28/)). The firm was registered in August 2023 with 7 billion yuan of capital and two state shareholders, Shanghai Electric Holding and a Shanghai International Trust subsidiary ([source](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/china-starts-production-home-grown-immersion-duv-chipmaking-tools-source-2026-07-28/)). It has no website and has taken in teams from SMEE, China's older scanner maker, and the startup Yuliangsheng ([source](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/china-starts-production-home-grown-immersion-duv-chipmaking-tools-source-2026-07-28/)). SMEE is strongest in i-line, the coarsest kind of scanner, and even there holds about four percent of the world market ([source](https://www.csis.org/blogs/strategic-technologies-blog/breakthroughs-or-boasts-assessing-recent-chinese-lithography)).

On EUV there is less evidence. Claims of Huawei mass production in 2026 remain unverified. Reuters reported in December 2025 that a Shenzhen team of former ASML engineers had built a prototype that made extreme ultraviolet light but had printed no chip ([source](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/how-china-built-its-manhattan-project-rival-west-ai-chips-2025-12-17/)). To print a chip, a machine also needs mirrors that carry that light to the wafer, and ASML's sole supplier of those mirrors is Zeiss ([source](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/937966/000162828026011377/asml-2025xannualxreportx.htm)).

## The chokepoint

If ASML stopped shipping, no fab anywhere could add EUV scanners. Tools already in fabs would keep printing for as long as they were serviced. In its 2025 annual report ASML said about 95 percent of the systems it had sold in the previous 30 years were still in use ([source](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/937966/000162828026011377/asml-2025xannualxreportx.htm)). It guarantees service and spare parts for each platform until at least a committed date, subject to export controls ([source](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/937966/000162828026011377/asml-2025xannualxreportx.htm)). ASML itself could not quickly rebuild its production, because Zeiss makes its optics and Trumpf its drive laser.

In July 2026 ASML put its 2026 capacity at around 65 standard (low-NA) EUV scanners and said it plans to add 30 percent for 2027 ([source](https://ourbrand.asml.com/asset/c8dbf3fc-4c5e-4406-83f6-27694b138245/Press-Release-Financial-Results-Q2-2026.pdf)).

For AI accelerators, EUV is the point of control, because it prints the finest layers, the ones that set how densely transistors and wires pack ([source](https://www.asml.com/en/products/euv-lithography-systems)). TSMC expects the number of layers needing High-NA to rise as AI designs grow more complex ([source](https://pr.tsmc.com/english/news/3338)).

## Takeaways

ASML also holds above 80 percent of the market for deep ultraviolet (DUV) lithography, the older kind that uses longer-wavelength light ([source](https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/250-million-asml-printer-behind-nvidias-chips-2026-07-28/)). ASML says the production capacity of Zeiss, its sole supplier of optics, limits how many lithography systems it can build ([source](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/937966/000162828026011377/asml-2025xannualxreportx.htm)).

Export controls bar extreme ultraviolet (EUV) scanners from Chinese chip foundries ([source](https://www.csis.org/blogs/strategic-technologies-blog/breakthroughs-or-boasts-assessing-recent-chinese-lithography)). In December 2025 Reuters reported a Chinese prototype that had made EUV light but had printed no chip ([source](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/how-china-built-its-manhattan-project-rival-west-ai-chips-2025-12-17/)). In July 2026 Reuters reported, citing one unnamed source, that a Shanghai firm with two state shareholders has started producing immersion DUV tools, which shine their light through water ([source](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/china-starts-production-home-grown-immersion-duv-chipmaking-tools-source-2026-07-28/)).
